# CORE OBSERVER QUESTIONS

## PURPOSE

This document defines the primary observer-side continuity
questions used throughout the HACR Hybrid Observatory.

The Observatory does not determine legitimacy,
authorize execution, or enforce operational outcomes.

The Observatory instead attempts to preserve visibility into:
- transition behavior
- continuity validity
- recoverability conditions

under changing topology and stress conditions.

This document is observational only.

---

# CORE REDUCTION

The Observatory does not preserve continuity.

The Observatory preserves the visibility of whether continuity
remains interpretable.

---

# OBSERVER QUESTION 1

## Is the transition still legible?

Transition legibility concerns whether transition behavior remains:
- observable
- bounded
- interpretable
- recoverable

under stress, degradation, interruption, or topology change.

Related layer:
- `TRANSITION_LEGIBILITY.md`

---

# OBSERVER QUESTION 2

## Is continuity still presently valid?

Anti-drift continuity concerns whether continuity:
- remains presently supported
- avoids stale-state inheritance
- preserves relation validity
- maintains replay visibility
- avoids hidden continuity drift

under changing conditions.

Related layer:
- `ANTI_DRIFT_CONTINUITY.md`

---

# OBSERVER QUESTION 3

## Is recoverability still observable?

Recoverability observability concerns whether:
- rollback remains visible
- degradation remains bounded
- interruption viability remains inspectable
- safe-state return potential remains observable

under current topology conditions.

Related layers:
- `TRANSITION_LEGIBILITY.md`
- `OUTPUT_SEMANTICS.md`

---

# RELATION VISIBILITY

The Observatory primarily inspects:
- relation survivability
- dependency continuity
- topology-local interpretation
- transition geometry

rather than static entities alone.

Continuity may degrade through relation instability
before visible collapse appears.

---

# OBSERVER BOUNDARY

The Observatory:
- observes
- classifies
- maps
- visualizes
- pressure-tests continuity behavior

The Observatory does not:
- govern
- authorize
- certify
- operationalize execution
- replace human judgment

All outputs remain observational only.

---

# NON-CLAIM

Observer questions:
- do not determine legitimacy
- do not guarantee recoverability
- do not certify safety
- do not imply execution validity

All observations remain bounded to:
- visible topology
- available evidence
- supplied inputs
- local inspection conditions

Unknown downstream conditions may remain possible.
