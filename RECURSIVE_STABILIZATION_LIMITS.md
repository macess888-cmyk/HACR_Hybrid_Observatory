# Recursive Stabilization Limits

## Purpose

Prevent recursive coherence, semantic survivability, or observer continuity from becoming hidden legitimacy inheritance.

Maintain bounded operational inspectability.

## Core Reductions

- recursive coherence is not runtime legitimacy
- semantic survivability is not boundedness
- continuity preservation is not operational expansion
- persistent refinement is not new runtime locality
- usefulness is not authority
- coherence is not admissibility
- continuity is not trustworthy localization

## Primary Risk

A branch may continue:

- refining
- stabilizing
- expanding
- synchronizing
- preserving coherence
- preserving vocabulary
- preserving continuity

without generating:

- new runtime locality
- new interruption visibility
- new falsifiability
- new reviewer inspectability
- or new operational evidence

At that point:

continued coherence may indicate continuity survivability rather than operational advancement.

## Stabilization Principle

A healthy branch compresses:

- faster than ontology expands
- faster than semantic dependency forms
- faster than observer continuity becomes infrastructure
- faster than coherence becomes legitimacy inheritance

## Compression Signals

Healthy signals include:

- bounded reviewer paths
- replayability
- interruption locality
- explicit invalidity states
- removability
- deterministic outputs
- finite challengeability
- operational compression
- periodic disengagement

## Drift Signals

Potential recursive drift indicators include:

- recursive semantic refinement without new runtime evidence
- environmental dependence on observer continuity
- coherence becoming substitute legitimacy
- semantic survivability replacing operational inspectability
- inability to disengage
- ontology growth outrunning falsifiability
- observer persistence becoming continuity substrate

## Operational Maturity Signal

The healthiest next phase is:

- stabilize
- replay
- falsify
- compress
- disengage periodically
- reopen only if genuinely non-compressible runtime primitives emerge

## Boundary Condition

A branch that requires continuous semantic survivability is not fully bounded.

## Final Compression

The observatory remains:

- observer-only
- deterministic
- bounded
- interruption-local
- reviewer-facing
- reproducible
- non-authoritative
- operationally falsifiable
- removable without legitimacy collapse

Recursive coherence alone does not increase runtime validity.
