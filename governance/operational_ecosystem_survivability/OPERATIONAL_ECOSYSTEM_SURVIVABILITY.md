# Operational Ecosystem Survivability

## Core Reduction

Traditional sustainment optimization does not automatically produce operational ecosystem survivability.

Operational continuity under contested execution conditions increasingly depends upon:

- synchronization survivability
- dependency resilience
- communications continuity
- industrial fallback capability
- operational trust continuity
- degraded coordination viability
- reconstruction feasibility
- interruption arrival viability

## Boundary

This repository does not perform:

- governance
- operational authorization
- execution control
- military orchestration
- strategic targeting
- command-and-control

This repository remains:

- observer-only
- runtime-local
- continuity-inspection-oriented
- non-authoritative
- operationally bounded

## Pressure Surface

Modern operational environments increasingly become:

- interconnected
- machine-assisted
- synchronization-sensitive
- dependency-dense
- visibility-exposed
- continuity-fragile
- operationally compressed

Meaning survivability may increasingly depend not only upon system capability itself, but whether surrounding operational conditions remain survivable under degradation pressure.

## Observatory Direction

The observatory therefore focuses on:

- survivability observability
- dependency topology inspection
- continuity degradation localization
- synchronization survivability analysis
- contested-runtime reconstruction pressure
- visibility-to-exposure pressure analysis
