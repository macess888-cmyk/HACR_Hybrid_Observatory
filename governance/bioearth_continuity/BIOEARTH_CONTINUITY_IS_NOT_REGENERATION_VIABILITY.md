# BioEarth Continuity Is Not Regeneration Viability

## Boundary

This repository treats biological and Earth-system continuity as observer-local diagnostic surfaces only.

Observable persistence is not proof of preserved regeneration viability.

## Core Reductions

- planetary continuity != biological recoverability
- life persistence != ecosystem recoverability
- habitat continuity != habitat viability
- biological motion != biological resilience
- species survival != reproductive continuity
- environmental observability != regeneration viability
- hydrological continuity != potable recoverability
- atmospheric continuity != breathable locality preservation
- geological stability appearance != preserved ecological coupling

## Inspection Claim

Biological and Earth systems may remain observable, active, measurable, and apparently coherent while regeneration corridors narrow beneath continuity persistence.

The inspected question is not:

> Is the system safe, healthy, governed, certified, or recoverable?

The inspected question is:

> Can regeneration viability remain materially present under continued biological and Earth-system continuity?

## Non-Claims

This note does not provide:

- ecological certification
- biological certification
- climate-policy instruction
- conservation authority
- intervention authorization
- prediction
- environmental management guidance
- species-risk classification
- operational command semantics

UNKNOWN -> HOLD.

No present-state proof of regeneration viability means no regeneration claim.
