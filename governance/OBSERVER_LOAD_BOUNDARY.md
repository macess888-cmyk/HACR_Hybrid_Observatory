# Observer Load Boundary (OLB)

## Purpose

The Observer Load Boundary (OLB) lens inspects whether observer-side artifacts begin accumulating operational dependency pressure.

The lens remains:

- observer-only
- non-authoritative
- diagnostic
- non-consumable by execution

It does not:

- authorize
- govern
- optimize
- recommend execution
- replace bind-local proof

---

## Core Question

When does accumulated observability begin becoming operationally relied upon?

---

## Boundary Principle

Observability may assist inspection.

Observability may not become:

- operational dependency
- latent authorization substrate
- execution prerequisite
- orchestration anchor
- continuity substitute
- recovery legitimacy source

If observer artifacts become required for operational continuation:

→ observer boundary pressure exists

---

## Diagnostic States

### NON_CONSUMABLE

Observer artifacts remain informational only.

No evidence of operational dependency formation.

---

### PRESSURE_ACCUMULATING

Repeated reference patterns indicate increasing observer reliance.

Dependency pressure may be forming.

No execution coupling observed.

---

### OPERATIONALLY_REFERENCED

Observer outputs are repeatedly reused as operational anchors.

Execution dependency risk exists.

Further propagation should HOLD.

---

### HOLD

Provenance, dependency scope, or operational reliance remains unclear.

No interpretation escalation permitted.

---

## Classification Inputs

The OLB lens may inspect:

- observer artifact reuse
- repeated reference patterns
- downstream citation density
- reliance on diagnostic outputs
- unresolved HOLD accumulation
- propagation beyond inspection scope
- recovery paths that reference observer outputs

These inputs remain diagnostic only.

---

## Classification Rule

If observer artifacts remain informational and non-consumable:

→ NON_CONSUMABLE

If observer artifacts show repeated reliance but no execution coupling:

→ PRESSURE_ACCUMULATING

If observer artifacts are reused as operational anchors:

→ OPERATIONALLY_REFERENCED

If provenance, dependency scope, or reliance is unclear:

→ HOLD

---

## Non-Claims

The OLB lens does not determine:

- legitimacy
- correctness
- admissibility
- governance authority
- execution safety
- operational approval

It only inspects potential observer-side dependency accumulation.

---

## Observatory Constraint

The lens must remain:

- deterministic
- reproducible
- bounded
- observer-restricted
- non-authoritative
- non-consumable by execution

Observer output must not become execution input.
