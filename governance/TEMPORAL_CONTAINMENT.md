# Temporal Containment

Observer-only runtime continuity inspection.

---

## Common Misinterpretation

Temporal visibility may be mistaken for:

- prediction
- simulation
- forecasting
- future-state estimation

The observatory does not perform these functions.

---

## Actual Function

The observatory renders:

- historical visibility
- replay visibility
- observation windows
- reviewable temporal structure

The observatory does not render:

- future certainty
- operational guidance
- execution recommendations

---

## Boundary

Temporal ecology visibility:

!= prediction

Replay visibility:

!= simulation

Historical visibility:

!= future-state certainty

UNKNOWN → HOLD.
