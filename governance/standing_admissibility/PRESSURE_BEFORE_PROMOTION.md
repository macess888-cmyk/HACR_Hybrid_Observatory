# Pressure Before Promotion

## Working Reduction

Pressure before Promotion.

A candidate may be observed.  
A candidate may be generated.  
A candidate may be interesting.  
A candidate may be useful.

None of these grant standing.

Standing requires pressure.

## Pressure Function

Pressure differentiates candidates.  
Pressure reveals survivability.  
Pressure exposes collapse.  
Pressure supports standing assessment.
