# Standing Acquisition Path v0.1

## Entry State

UNKNOWN

Unknown is admissible.  
Unknown is not failure.  
Unknown does not authorize action.

## Step 1 — Observation

UNKNOWN  
↓  
OBSERVE

Observation reveals candidate structures.

Observation does not create determination.  
Observation does not create standing.  
Observation does not create authority.

## Step 2 — Candidate Generation

OBSERVATIONS  
↓  
GENERATE CANDIDATES

Candidates may include:

A  
B  
C  
...  
N

Candidates possess no standing by default.

## Step 3 — Pressure

CANDIDATES  
↓  
PRESSURE

Pressure differentiates candidates.

Pressure is diagnostic.  
Pressure is not promotion.  
Pressure is not authority.  
Pressure is survivability inspection.

## Step 4 — Observe Response

PRESSURE  
↓  
OBSERVE RESPONSE

Possible responses:

SURVIVES  
DEGRADES  
COLLAPSES  
AMBIGUOUS

## Step 5 — Assess Standing

RESPONSE  
↓  
ASSESS STANDING

Possible outcomes:

Standing Established  
Standing Rejected  
Standing Unresolved

## Step 6 — Terminal Outcome

Standing Established → PROCEED  
Standing Rejected → REFUSE  
Standing Unresolved → HOLD

## Hold Loop

HOLD  
↓  
Observe More  
↓  
Generate Better Candidate  
↓  
Apply New Pressure  
↓  
Reassess Standing

## Core Discipline

Pressure before Promotion.  
Candidate ≠ Standing.  
Standing ≠ Authority.  
Authority ≠ Action.  
Failure ≠ Replacement.  
Unknown ≠ Error.

## Compressed Form

Pressure distinguishes.  
Standing decides.  
Promotion follows.

UNKNOWN → HOLD.
