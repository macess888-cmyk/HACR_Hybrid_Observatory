# Self-Correcting Observability Boundary

## Purpose

This document stabilizes the boundary between:

observer-only self-correcting inspection

and:

self-governing execution infrastructure.

---

# Observatory Boundary

The Observatory may:

- localize ambiguity
- compress runtime reductions
- detect semantic drift
- map representational divergence
- pressure interruption realism
- and stabilize operational falsifiability.

The Observatory may NOT:

- govern execution
- authorize intervention
- operationalize interruption
- automate consequence
- create authority inheritance
- or silently become execution infrastructure.
