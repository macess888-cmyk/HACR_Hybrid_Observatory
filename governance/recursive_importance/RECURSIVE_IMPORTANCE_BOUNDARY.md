# Recursive Importance Boundary

Observability utility does not create historical necessity.

This boundary inspects whether the observatory begins treating itself as uniquely necessary, civilization-critical, indispensable, or historically central through recursive explanatory coherence.

The Recursive Importance Drift Auditor is observer-only.

It does not validate importance, assign legitimacy, certify originality, or determine social value.

## Boundary Condition

The observatory may inspect runtime and semantic pressure surfaces.
