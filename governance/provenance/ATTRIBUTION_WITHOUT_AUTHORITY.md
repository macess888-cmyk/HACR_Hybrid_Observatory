# Attribution Without Authority

## Purpose

This document stabilizes attribution without drifting into authority inheritance.

---

# Runtime Concern

Repositories may gradually inherit:

- symbolic legitimacy
- reputational authority
- conceptual governance
- or atmospheric execution expectation

through attribution persistence alone.

---

# Runtime Goal

Allow:

- provenance
- authorship
- continuity
- and repository traceability

WITHOUT inheriting:

- governance authority
- execution legitimacy
- intervention authority
- or operational sovereignty.

---

# Strong Runtime Reduction

Attribution
!=
authority inheritance.

UNKNOWN -> HOLD.
