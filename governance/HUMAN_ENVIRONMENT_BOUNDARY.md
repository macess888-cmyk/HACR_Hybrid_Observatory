# HUMAN ENVIRONMENT BOUNDARY

## Purpose

Clarify why deterministic validation semantics cannot transfer directly into uncontrolled human environments.

---

## Core Distinction

Execution-bound systems:
- bounded variables
- isolated transitions
- constrained execution surfaces
- reproducible state

Human/public environments:
- uncontrolled variables
- overlapping causality
- non-isolated interaction surfaces
- non-deterministic behavioral interpretation

Therefore:

Human observational environments cannot inherit deterministic proof semantics cleanly.

---

## Reduction

Observed behavior:
!=
Verified mechanism.

Anomaly recurrence:
!=
Admissible execution origin.

Behavioral interpretation:
!=
Execution-surface-local proof.

---

## Observatory Restriction

The observatory may:
- observe
- localize
- classify
- pressure-test

The observatory may NOT:
- infer hidden intent
- infer coordinated monitoring
- elevate social salience into operational proof
- collapse ambiguity into causality

---

## Fail-Closed Requirement

Ambiguous human-environment interpretation:
→ HOLD

No authority may emerge from observational amplification.
