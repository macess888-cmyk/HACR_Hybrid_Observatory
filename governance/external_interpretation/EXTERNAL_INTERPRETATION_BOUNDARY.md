# External Interpretation Boundary

External readability does not guarantee bounded interpretation.

This boundary inspects whether observability artifacts may be misread by external audiences as conspiracy, prediction, governance authority, ideology, hidden-world explanation, inevitability architecture, or intervention logic.

The External Interpretation Risk Scanner is observer-only.

It does not determine intent, truth, audience response, or public meaning.

It only localizes likely misread pathways.

## Core Reductions

public clarity
!=
bounded interpretation

external interest
!=
authority inheritance

reader resonance
!=
semantic safety

misread risk
!=
author intent

interpretability
!=
governance validity

## Pressure Surfaces

The scanner inspects for:

- conspiracy-adjacent readings
- hidden-control interpretation
- prediction-engine interpretation
- governance-authority interpretation
- ideology/doctrine interpretation
- inevitability framing
- civilization-scale totalization
- intervention logic
- public overclaim risk

## Boundary Condition

All public-facing observability artifacts should remain:

- bounded
- non-authoritative
- non-predictive
- non-remediative
- non-ideological
- locally testable
- observer-only

If external interpretation risk becomes unresolved:

UNKNOWN -> HOLD.

Break survivability, not ontology.
