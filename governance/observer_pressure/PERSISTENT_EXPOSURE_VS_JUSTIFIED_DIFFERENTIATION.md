# Persistent Exposure vs Justified Differentiation

## Core Reduction

Persistent exposure != justified differentiation.

Repeated survivability under inspection does not independently justify:

- ontology expansion
- object multiplication
- semantic canonization
- independent authority surfaces
- new primitive declaration
- categorization inflation

## Pressure Surface

Inspection geometry may remain:

- coherent
- compression-stable
- localization-preserving
- constraint-consistent

while observer-side distinction pressure progressively accumulates through:

survivability
↓
familiarity
↓
distinction pressure
↓
semantic expansion pressure

without requiring inspection invalidity.

## Important Separation

Compression stability != categorization stability.

Observer familiarity accumulation may increase perceived object legitimacy even while compression remains operationally valid.

## Observatory Boundary

The observatory:

- detects pressure
- localizes pressure
- records pressure
- visualizes pressure

but does not:

- canonize pressure
- authorize ontology
- grant independent object legitimacy
- manufacture semantic expansion

## HOLD Preservation

Unresolved survivability pressure must remain eligible for HOLD containment.

Repeated exposure alone must not silently inherit semantic authorization.

UNKNOWN -> HOLD.
