# Deterministic Replay Stabilization Note

## Purpose

This note records a bounded operational pattern:

stable replay surfaces
may reduce
continuity ambiguity
under degraded coordination conditions.

---

## Observed operational behavior

Replay verification may remain stable while:
- interface visibility degrades
- indexing fails
- observer confidence fluctuates
- synchronization quality decreases.

Example bounded replay anchors:

- deterministic artifact hashes
- reproducible exports
- local verification
- replay validation receipts.

---

## Boundary

Replay stability does not imply:
- correctness
- governance legitimacy
- survivability guarantees
- interruption impossibility.

Replay stability only indicates bounded reconstruction consistency.
