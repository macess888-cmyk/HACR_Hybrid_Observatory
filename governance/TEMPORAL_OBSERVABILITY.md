# Temporal Observability

Observer-only runtime continuity inspection.

---

## Purpose

Temporal observability provides visibility into how observer-visible environments change across reviewable observation windows.

Temporal observability does not predict future states.

Temporal observability does not establish causality.

Temporal observability does not establish governance validity.

---

## Question

Temporal observability answers:

"How did the observer-visible environment change?"

It does not answer:

"What will happen next?"

---

## Examples

Examples include:

- replay windows
- historical topology visibility
- ecology drift visibility
- temporal layer stacks
- replay trajectories

---

## Containment

Temporal visibility is not prediction.

Temporal visibility is not simulation.

Temporal visibility is not forecasting.

Temporal visibility remains observer-only.
