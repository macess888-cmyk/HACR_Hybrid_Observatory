# Minimal Review Surface

## Purpose

The observatory pressures whether operational clarity survives reduction.

## Core Reduction

More observability
≠
more clarity.

## Mature Runtime Concern

Systems may accumulate:

- dashboards
- workflows
- reports
- visualizations
- tooling
- procedural layers

while bounded comprehension progressively collapses.

## Observatory Direction

Healthy maturity should preserve:

- minimal traversal burden
- deterministic reproducibility
- bounded falsifiability
- repair-path accessibility
- renderer independence
- and operational clarity density.

## Strong Reduction

Operational clarity should survive simplification.

## Non-Claims

This note does NOT:

- reject tooling
- reject visualization
- reject diagnostics
- replace operators

Observer-only bounded inspection.
