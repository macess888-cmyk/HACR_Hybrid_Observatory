# Direct Human Behavior Observatory HOLD

## Boundary

Direct human-behavior observatory expansion is intentionally held.

This repository does not model, score, predict, optimize, manipulate, govern, or classify human behavior.

## Reason for HOLD

Human-behavior inspection carries elevated semantic risk because observer-local diagnostics can drift into:

- behavioral authority
- persuasion optimization
- manipulation
- human scoring
- psychometric inference
- predictive enforcement
- institutional control semantics
- legitimacy assignment

## Allowed Adjacent Scope

The repository may inspect system-level continuity surfaces such as:

- documentation continuity
- receipt lineage continuity
- reproducibility continuity
- topology rendering continuity
- institutional artifact continuity
- observer-local interpretive drift in artifacts

## Disallowed Scope

The repository does not provide:

- human behavior prediction
- psychological classification
- persuasion strategy
- compliance optimization
- user scoring
- social governance
- behavioral intervention authority
- manipulation systems

## Operating Rule

UNKNOWN -> HOLD.

Direct human-behavior expansion remains HOLD unless future work can preserve observer-only, non-authoritative, non-manipulative, non-classificatory boundaries.
