# Runtime Reconstructability Boundaries

## Purpose

This document clarifies bounded runtime-observability constraints around:

- continuation persistence
- interruption survivability
- reconstruction conditions
- post-failure observability
- occupation pressure
- non-finality

This document does NOT introduce:

- new governance primitives
- continuity certification
- survivability guarantees
- inevitability claims
- authority expansion
- restoration semantics

The observatory remains:

- observer-restricted
- runtime-bound
- interruption-local
- falsifiable
- non-authoritative

---

# Core Runtime Clarifications

## Occupation != Finality

Continuation occupation of operational reality does not independently establish:

- irreversibility
- inevitability
- permanent closure
- interruption impossibility
- terminal continuation dominance

Continuation persistence may increase operational pressure without proving finality.

Finality must remain independently demonstrable.

---

## Continuation Persistence != Legitimacy

Continuation persistence after interruption degradation or interruption failure does not independently establish:

- validity
- legitimacy
- recoverability
- correctness
- operational stability
- governance sufficiency

Observable persistence is not continuity certification.

---

## FAIL != Continuation Collapse

FAIL localizes interruption conditions only.

FAIL does not independently establish:

- continuation termination
- system collapse
- governance collapse
- coordination collapse
- continuity invalidation
- restoration impossibility

FAIL remains interruption-local.

---

## Observable Persistence != Validated Persistence

Continued observability after interruption degradation does not independently establish:

- trusted continuity
- acceptable survivability
- operational legitimacy
- resilient recoverability
- restoration viability

Visibility survival is not continuity endorsement.

---

# Post-FAIL Inspection Boundary

Post-FAIL inspection remains bounded to observability conditions only.

Post-FAIL inspection may observe:

- operational persistence
- continued coordination
- execution continuity
- distributed continuation
- synchronization persistence
- observable survivability pockets

without independently establishing:

- stability
- legitimacy
- recoverability
- governance validity
- interruption impossibility
- continuation finality

The observatory does not certify continuation meaning.

---

# Reconstruction Clarification

Continuation pressure accumulation does not independently eliminate the possibility of interruption reconstruction.

Continuation compounding does not independently prove:

- permanent occupation
- irreversible hardening
- interruption death
- synchronization totality
- reconstruction impossibility

Interruption reconstructability remains runtime-testable and condition-dependent.

---

# HOLD Clarification

UNKNOWN -> HOLD remains the correct bounded resolution under unresolved runtime conditions.

HOLD does not imply:

- stability
- approval
- inevitability
- continuity validation
- interruption invalidation

HOLD preserves bounded uncertainty under incomplete runtime determination.

---

# Observatory Boundary

The observatory inspects runtime survivability conditions only.

The observatory does not:

- govern continuation
- certify survivability
- determine legitimacy
- authorize persistence
- declare inevitability
- assess metaphysical permanence

The observatory remains inspection-local and observer-restricted.
