# PROPAGATION GATING AND ISOLATED VISIBILITY

## PURPOSE

This note formalizes a bounded observability condition where:

visibility may propagate,
while execution significance remains gated.

This document is observer-only.

---

## CORE REDUCTION

Visibility may propagate.

Execution significance may not.

---

## PROPAGATION CONDITIONS

Observation may be shared across bounded interfaces.

Propagation does not imply:

- execution continuity
- authority inheritance
- admissibility transfer
- governance legitimacy
- downstream activation rights

---

## ISOLATED INSTANCE CONDITIONS

Instances remain observationally isolated.

Parallel visibility does not become:

- aggregate authority
- consensus
- inevitability
- continuity proof
- topology endorsement

No cross-instance authority inheritance is permitted.

---

## GATING CONDITIONS

Propagation requires:

- explicit compatibility
- bounded containment
- local observability
- reversible exposure

If provenance,
containment,
or recovery viability becomes unclear:

HOLD.

---

## OBSERVATORY POSITION

The observatory may expose:

- routing conditions
- compatibility boundaries
- local conflicts
- visibility persistence

only.

The observatory may not:

- synchronize interpretation
- unify semantic state
- establish operational hierarchy
- imply admissible continuation

UNKNOWN → HOLD
