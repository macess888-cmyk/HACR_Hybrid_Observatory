# Visibility Without Survivability

## Core Reduction

Visibility optimization does not automatically produce survivability.

In highly interconnected operational ecosystems:

increased observability
may simultaneously increase:
- operational exposure
- dependency visibility
- synchronization inference
- bottleneck localization
- tempo reconstruction capability
- continuity attackability

## Pressure Surface

Operational visibility systems may unintentionally expose:

- readiness degradation patterns
- replenishment bottlenecks
- communications fragility
- maintenance concentration
- industrial dependencies
- coordination failure corridors

if surrounding ecosystems become compromised, intercepted, degraded, or externally observable.

## Observatory Constraint

This repository does not evaluate strategic legitimacy.

This repository only observes:

- continuity survivability pressure
- visibility-exposure relationships
- synchronization survivability
- reconstruction feasibility
- operational dependency concentration

## Stabilization Boundary

Visibility itself must not silently inherit:
- trust
- legitimacy
- survivability assumptions
- operational correctness
- continuity guarantees

persistent visibility != operational survivability

UNKNOWN -> HOLD.
