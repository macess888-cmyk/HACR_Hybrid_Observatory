# TOPOLOGY_INDEPENDENT_SURVIVABILITY

## Purpose

Observer-restricted inspection note describing whether continuation
survives topology substitution.

Topology substitution may include:
- infrastructure replacement
- operator replacement
- institutional replacement
- language/environment replacement
- degraded runtime conditions

---

## Core Reduction

If continuation survives across materially different topologies,
the continuation may possess topology-independent survivability.

---

## Observatory Focus

This note does NOT:
- classify legitimacy
- determine admissibility
- certify persistence correctness

It only localizes whether continuation degrades under substitution pressure.

---

## Important Boundary

Replication across targets does not inherit authority.

Replication only increases observability confidence regarding:
- survivability
- reconstructability
- continuation resilience
