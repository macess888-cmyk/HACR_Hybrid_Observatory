# EXECUTABLE INTERRUPTION CONSTRUCTABILITY

## Runtime Concern

Interruption may remain:

- visible,
- semantically preserved,
- procedurally represented,
- escalation-visible,
- and challengeable

after executable interruption itself has become operationally non-constructable under continuation pressure.

## Core Runtime Reduction

Semantic interruptibility != executable interruption constructability.

## Pressure Focus

The observatory increasingly pressures whether interruption can still:

- operationally arrive,
- synchronize,
- localize,
- reconstruct,
- traverse,
- and materially affect runtime consequence.

## Boundary

The observatory:

- does not authorize interruption,
- does not determine legitimacy,
- does not execute governance,
- and does not replace operational authority.

Observer-only.
