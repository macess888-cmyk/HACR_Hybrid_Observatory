# Continuity Hierarchy Note

## Purpose

This note records a bounded observability distinction:

runtime continuity
may survive
far beyond
human-visible continuity confidence.

This note does not claim:
- inevitability
- recoverability
- correctness
- governance legitimacy
- survivability guarantees.

It only records that continuity visibility layers may degrade asymmetrically.

---

## Observed hierarchy

Example bounded continuity hierarchy:

UI visibility
<
search visibility
<
service indexing
<
artifact availability
<
deterministic replay
<
local verification.

Higher observability layers may fail while lower continuity layers remain operational.

---

## Operational interpretation

This may produce conditions where:
- observer confidence collapses
- interface visibility degrades
- indexing becomes unavailable
- search layers fail

while:
- replay remains valid
- artifact integrity persists
- local verification remains reconstructible.

---

## Boundary

This note remains:
- observer-only
- diagnostic-only
- non-authoritative
- non-predictive.
