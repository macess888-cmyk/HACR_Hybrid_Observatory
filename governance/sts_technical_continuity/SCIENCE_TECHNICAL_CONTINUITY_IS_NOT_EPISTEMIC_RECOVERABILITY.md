# Science / Technical Continuity Is Not Epistemic Recoverability

## Boundary

This repository treats scientific, technical, and knowledge-infrastructure continuity as observer-local diagnostic surfaces only.

Technical continuity is not proof of epistemic recoverability.

## Core Reductions

- technical continuity != epistemic recoverability
- publication continuity != knowledge recoverability
- citation density != evidence continuity
- benchmark performance != field validity
- standard adoption != technical legitimacy
- replication presence != reproducibility health
- toolchain continuity != method recoverability
- dataset persistence != evidence grounding
- peer review != truth closure
- technical adoption != epistemic resolution
- field momentum != correction viability

## Inspection Claim

A scientific or technical field may remain active, published, cited, funded, standardized, benchmarked, or deployed while its ability to reproduce, correct, localize, or independently recover claims degrades beneath continuity persistence.

The inspected question is not:

> Is the field true, valid, legitimate, authoritative, or settled?

The inspected question is:

> Can epistemic recoverability remain materially viable under continued scientific or technical continuity?

## Non-Claims

This note does not provide:

- truth determination
- expert legitimacy assignment
- field validity judgment
- policy instruction
- institutional authority
- scientific certification
- technical certification
- benchmark certification
- research ranking
- prediction
- governance authority

## Operating Rule

UNKNOWN -> HOLD.

No present-state proof of recoverability means no epistemic recoverability claim.

Observer-only.
