# STABILIZATION_PRESSURE_OBSERVABILITY

## Purpose

Observer-restricted note inspecting how unresolved exposure,
repetition, familiarity, and operational continuity influence
stabilization pressure over time.

---

## Core Reduction

Stabilization pressure may accumulate independently from
formal authority determination.

---

## Potential Pressure Surfaces

- familiarity accumulation
- confidence inheritance
- operational normalization
- replay vitality degradation
- interruption synchronization fatigue
- elapsed-time stabilization
- topology hardening

---

## Important Boundary

Observable stabilization pressure does NOT imply:
- legitimacy
- correctness
- inevitability
- governance closure

---

## Observatory Position

This note only localizes whether interruption viability,
challengeability, or destabilization capacity degrades under
repeated unresolved exposure.
