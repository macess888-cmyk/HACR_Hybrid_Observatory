# CANDIDATE VOLUME VS LOCALIZATION

## Reduction

An increase in generated candidates does not inherently imply improved localization capability.

Systems may appear analytically active while remaining localization-static.

---

# Boundary

Candidate multiplication must not inherit:

- evidentiary improvement
- convergence implication
- analytical maturity
- resolution proximity

unless rejection capability measurably improves.

---

# Observatory Interpretation

Large output surfaces may increase:

- operator continuation pressure
- apparent momentum
- perceived analytical depth
- confidence inheritance

without increasing:

- discrimination quality
- localization precision
- reproducibility strength
- rejection integrity

---

# HOLD Condition

If localization quality remains unchanged despite candidate expansion:

HOLD

is the correct bounded observatory state.
