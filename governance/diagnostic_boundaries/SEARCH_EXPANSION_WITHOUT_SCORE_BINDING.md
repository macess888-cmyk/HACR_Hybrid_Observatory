# SEARCH EXPANSION WITHOUT SCORE BINDING

## Purpose

This note documents a bounded observability failure mode where diagnostic expansion increases candidate volume without increasing localization, discrimination, or rejection quality.

The purpose is not to evaluate cryptography, optimization quality, or analytical correctness.

The purpose is to localize a general observability instability:

search expansion may create operational continuation pressure without producing evidence gain.

---

# Core Reduction

More probes do not inherently produce more observability.

More transforms do not inherently produce more evidence.

More candidates do not inherently produce more localization.

If rejection capability does not improve alongside expansion capability, the system may become operationally active while remaining observationally weak.

---

# Observability Failure Mode

A diagnostic harness may exhibit the following progression:

1. Initial ambiguity
2. Probe expansion
3. Candidate multiplication
4. Increased apparent activity
5. Increased continuation pressure
6. Reduced rejection clarity
7. Apparent momentum without localization improvement

In this condition:

output growth
!=
evidence growth

---

# Runtime Interpretation Boundary

Search expansion alone must not inherit:

- correctness
- convergence
- validation
- evidence sufficiency
- localization success
- operational certainty

Expansion only increases traversal surface.

It does not independently improve discrimination.

---

# HOLD Condition

If candidate volume increases while rejection quality remains unchanged, the correct observatory state is:

HOLD

not:

PASS

This preserves separation between:

activity
and
evidence.

---

# Diagnostic Stabilization Principle

No new transform family should be added unless the current scoring layer can explain:

- why prior candidates failed
- why new candidates are meaningfully distinguishable
- what rejection boundary improved

Without this constraint, expansion may become self-propagating continuation pressure.

---

# Reverse Probe

The critical reverse-analysis question is:

"What would have stopped expansion earlier?"

A stable observability boundary requires explicit rejection sufficiency before additional traversal complexity is introduced.

---

# Dynamic Probe

Diagnostic systems may continue evolving structurally while localization capability remains static.

This creates a condition where:

continuation survives,
while observability stagnates.

This condition is operationally important because apparent analytical motion may obscure unchanged evidence quality.

---

# Observatory Boundary

This note does NOT:

- certify analytical correctness
- evaluate cryptographic validity
- determine truth conditions
- authorize operational decisions
- claim convergence success

This note ONLY localizes a bounded observability instability involving search expansion without score-binding improvement.

---

# Output Semantics

PASS:
Rejection clarity improves alongside expansion.

HOLD:
Expansion increases while rejection capability remains unchanged.

FAIL:
Expansion reduces localization integrity or introduces unstable interpretation surfaces.

UNKNOWN:
Insufficient evidence to determine discrimination improvement.
