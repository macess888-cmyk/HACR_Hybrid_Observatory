# Core Reduction

Structural persistence does not guarantee operational equivalence.

A relational structure may remain reconstructable while:

- interruption survivability changes
- authority localization drifts
- reconstruction feasibility degrades
- consequence propagation shifts
- synchronization pressure accumulates
- recovery pathways collapse
- operational dependency concentration increases

Observed structural recurrence alone does not establish:

- legitimacy
- admissibility
- safety
- recoverability
- continuity survivability
- operational equivalence
- ontological truth

Reconstructability != admissibility.

Persistence != operational stability.
