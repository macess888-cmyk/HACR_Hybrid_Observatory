# Structural Persistence != Operational Equivalence

## Core Reduction

Structural persistence does not guarantee operational equivalence.

Relational structures may remain partially reconstructable across changing:

- terminology
- abstraction layers
- representation styles
- modalities
- recursive inspection conditions
- compression states
- synchronization environments

while operational consequence behavior materially changes underneath.

The observatory therefore separates:

- structural persistence
from
- operational equivalence.
