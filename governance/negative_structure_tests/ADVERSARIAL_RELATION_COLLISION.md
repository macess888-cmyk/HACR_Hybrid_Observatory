# Adversarial Relation Collision

## Core Reduction

Semantic similarity does not guarantee relational equivalence.

Relational similarity does not guarantee operational equivalence.

Systems may preserve recognizable surface structure while underlying dependency geometry, causality, authority localization, or interruption survivability materially differ.

The observatory therefore separates:

- semantic resemblance
from
- relational equivalence

and:

- relational persistence
from
- operational consequence equivalence.

---

# Collision Testing Purpose

Adversarial relation-collision testing inspects whether systems incorrectly infer:

- equivalence
- continuity
- legitimacy
- structural stability
- or operational similarity

from semantically adjacent but structurally divergent representations.

The goal is bounded falsifiability under transformation pressure.
