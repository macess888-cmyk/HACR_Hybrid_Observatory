# Expansion Boundary

Purpose:
Prevent survivability taxonomy proliferation and semantic inflation.

---

# Expansion Constraint

No new survivability category should be introduced unless:

- it represents a genuinely distinct execution primitive
- it cannot be compressed into existing replay, reactivation, lineage, or substitution surfaces
- it materially improves execution-bound falsifiability
- it reduces ambiguity more than it increases conceptual surface area

---

# Compression Priority

Prefer:
- primitive compression
- bounded execution concepts
- reviewer clarity
- falsifiable operational distinctions

Avoid:
- recursive taxonomy growth
- survivability ontology proliferation
- semantic density escalation
- explanation complexity growth

---

# Stabilization Principle

If a new category can be reduced to an existing execution primitive:
→ compress
→ consolidate
→ avoid new ontology layers

---

# Final Constraint

Operational falsifiability should grow faster than taxonomy complexity.
