# ANOMALY VS ADMISSIBLE CAUSALITY

## Purpose

Clarify the distinction between:
- observed anomaly
- admissible causal mechanism

The observatory may:
- detect behavioral anomalies
- localize unexpected transitions
- classify runtime deviations
- surface reproducible discontinuities

The observatory may NOT:
- infer hidden coordination
- infer actor intent
- infer distributed causality from salience alone
- elevate observational coincidence into admissible mechanism

---

## Core Reduction

Observed anomaly:
!=
Admissible causality.

Behavioral salience:
!=
Verified coordinated mechanism.

Replayable coincidence:
!=
Execution-bound proof.

---

## Boundary

Human/social environments:
- are non-isolated
- contain uncontrolled variables
- permit overlapping alternative causality
- cannot guarantee execution-surface locality

Therefore:

Observational recurrence alone does not satisfy:
- admissibility
- causality
- coordination proof
- bind-valid execution origin

---

## Observatory Restriction

The observatory remains:
- observer-only
- bounded
- artifact-driven
- fail-closed under ambiguity

UNKNOWN:
→ HOLD

INSUFFICIENT:
→ HOLD

No ontology expansion from partial signal.
