# Recovery Theater vs Recovery Reachability

## Core Reduction

Recovery appearance
≠
recovery reachability.

## Pressure Surface

A system may appear recoverable because:

- recovery tooling exists
- logs exist
- replay exists
- dashboards exist
- visual explanations exist
- procedural guidance exists

while actual deterministic recovery traversal remains:

- unclear
- fragmented
- inaccessible
- non-localized
- or operationally ambiguous.

## Observatory Direction

The observatory pressures whether:

deterministic recovery traversal remains reproducible independently of visual reassurance structures.

## Mature Runtime Concern

Recovery narratives may silently inherit:

- trust
- reassurance
- perceived resilience
- operational confidence

without preserving bounded repair accessibility.

## Non-Claims

This note does NOT:

- prove recovery capability
- prove resilience
- certify systems
- authorize repair
- replace operators

Observer-only bounded inspection.
