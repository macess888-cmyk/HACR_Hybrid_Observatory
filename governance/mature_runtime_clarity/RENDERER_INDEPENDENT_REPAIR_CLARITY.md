# Renderer-Independent Repair Clarity

## Purpose

This note pressures whether deterministic repair-path clarity remains reproducible when:

- SVG rendering fails
- screenshots disappear
- dashboards degrade
- visual coherence collapses
- onboarding smoothness is removed
- or reviewer reassurance layers become unavailable

The observatory does not assume:

visual continuity
=
operational clarity.

## Core Reduction

Visual continuity
≠
repair traversal clarity.

## Pressure Direction

A system may appear understandable because:

- rendering succeeds
- visuals remain smooth
- screenshots remain coherent
- onboarding remains simple
- dashboards remain aesthetically stable

while actual repair traversal:

- grows ambiguous
- becomes reviewer-hostile
- fragments across tooling
- or loses deterministic accessibility.

## Observatory Direction

The observatory pressures whether:

deterministic repair traversal remains reproducible without visual reassurance inheritance.

## Non-Claims

- prove recoverability
- prove safety
- prove governance

- replace human judgment
