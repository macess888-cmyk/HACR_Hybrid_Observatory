# Reviewer Burden as Failure Surface

## Core Reduction

High observability burden
may conceal
low interruption clarity.

## Pressure Surface

A system may appear mature because:

- tooling expands
- dashboards increase
- visualizations multiply
- workflows accumulate
- reports become comprehensive

while reviewer traversal cost silently grows beyond bounded inspection capability.

## Mature Runtime Concern

Excessive traversal burden may create:

- reassurance inheritance
- reviewer fatigue
- continuity normalization
- unchallenged assumptions
- procedural trust stabilization

without improving deterministic falsifiability.

## Observatory Direction

Healthy maturity reduces:

- reviewer traversal complexity
- onboarding friction
- repair ambiguity
- visualization dependence

while preserving bounded reproducibility.

## Non-Claims

This note does NOT:

- evaluate personnel
- assess competence
- operationalize review authority
- replace human oversight
