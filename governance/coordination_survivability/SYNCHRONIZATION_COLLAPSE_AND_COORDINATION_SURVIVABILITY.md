Operational ecosystems may fail not because nodes disappear, but because synchronization viability collapses.

- timing asymmetry
- degraded communications
- coordination latency
- distributed reconstruction delay
- interruption arrival mismatch
- machine/human timing divergence
- dependency synchronization collapse
