# Semantic Cooling Boundary

## Purpose

The Semantic Cooling Auditor exists to inspect whether observability artifacts begin accumulating semantic authority, ontology drift, interpretive survivability, or legitimacy inheritance through density, coherence, repetition, or aesthetic completeness.

The tool is observer-only.

The tool does not:

- prescribe governance
- assign legitimacy
- determine truth
- authorize execution
- resolve ontology
- create intervention mandates
- produce predictive certainty

The tool only localizes semantic survivability pressure surfaces.

## Pressure Surfaces

The auditor inspects for:

- authority drift
- ontology inflation
- semantic-density overload
- shadow mythology risk
- survivability-language recursion
- inevitability implication
- civilization-scale totalization
- aesthetic authority inheritance
- HOLD degradation
- falsifiability erosion

---

## Output Semantics

PASS
- bounded and compressible

COOL
- density increasing beyond stable compression

HOLD
- unresolved semantic survivability pressure

STOP
- ontology or authority drift detected

REWRITE
- artifact requires bounded reformulation

---

## Boundary Condition

All observability artifacts must remain:

- observer-local
- runtime-local
- falsifiability-oriented
- non-authoritative
- independently compressible
- execution-boundary reducible

If semantic survivability begins functioning as operational legitimacy:

UNKNOWN -> HOLD.

Break survivability, not ontology.
