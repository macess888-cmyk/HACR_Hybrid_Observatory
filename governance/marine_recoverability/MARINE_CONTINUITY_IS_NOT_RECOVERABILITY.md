# Marine Continuity Is Not Biological Recoverability

## Boundary

This repository treats marine and ocean-system continuity as an observer-local diagnostic surface only.

Continued ocean function is not proof of preserved ecological recovery.

## Core Reductions

- ocean continuity != ecological recoverability
- water movement != ecosystem health
- telemetry continuity != biological recoverability
- species presence != trophic stability
- productivity != regeneration capacity
- oxygen detected != usable habitat
- migration != adaptation
- reef persistence != reef recoverability
- observability != interruption realism

## Inspection Claim

A marine system may remain observable, circulating, biologically active, and visually coherent while its recoverability corridor narrows beneath continuity persistence.

The inspected question is not:

> Is the ecosystem valid, safe, healthy, managed, or certified?

The inspected question is:

> Can biological recoverability remain materially viable under continued ocean-system continuity?

## Non-Claims

This note does not provide:

- conservation authority
- fisheries guidance
- ecological certification
- climate-policy instruction
- species-risk classification
- intervention authorization
- prediction
- mission-control semantics
- management legitimacy

## Operating Rule

UNKNOWN -> HOLD.

No present-state proof of recoverability means no recoverability claim.
