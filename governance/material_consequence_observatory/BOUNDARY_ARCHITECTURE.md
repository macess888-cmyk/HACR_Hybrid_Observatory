# Material Consequence Observatory Boundary Architecture

## Allowed

The following are allowed:

- Consequence inspection
- Exposure mapping
- Degradation mapping
- Persistence mapping
- Dependency mapping
- Recoverability mapping
- Environmental interaction review
- Human and ecological impact review
- Known-unknown identification
- Receipt generation

## Not Allowed

The following are not allowed:

- Synthesis instructions
- Manufacturing instructions
- Optimization guidance
- Enhancement guidance
- Weaponization guidance
- Concealment guidance
- Delivery guidance
- Bypass guidance
- Hazard amplification
- Destructive capability generation
