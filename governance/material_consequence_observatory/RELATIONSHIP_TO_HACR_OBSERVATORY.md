## Purpose

This document defines the relationship between the Material Consequence Observatory and the broader HACR Hybrid Observatory.

The Material Consequence Observatory is not a separate governance system.

It is not a certification system.

It is not a material discovery system.

It is not a capability-generation system.

It is an observer-only extension of the broader observability posture.

UNKNOWN → HOLD.

---

## HACR Observatory Core Question

The HACR Hybrid Observatory asks:

> Can important operational properties diverge before visible consequence emerges?

The Observatory improves visibility into:

- continuity
- interruption
- recoverability
- signal ecology
- formation visibility
- reference preservation

without crossing into authority, decision, execution, or governance.

---

## Material Consequence Observatory Core Question

The Material Consequence Observatory asks:

> Can important material, ecological, human, or infrastructural consequences diverge before visible consequence emerges?

It improves visibility into:

- degradation
- exposure
- persistence
- dependency
- recoverability
- consequence accumulation

without crossing into synthesis, optimization, deployment, or capability generation.
