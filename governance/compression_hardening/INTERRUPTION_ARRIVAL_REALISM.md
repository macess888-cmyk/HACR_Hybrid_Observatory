# Interruption Arrival Realism

## Purpose

This document pressures whether interruption can still materially arrive before continuation hardens under practical runtime conditions.

---

# Runtime Concern

Modern systems increasingly preserve:

- representation
- replay
- telemetry
- observability
- governance visibility
- accountability
- escalation surfaces
- operational coherence
- and simulation continuity

while executable interruption increasingly becomes:

- delayed
- reconstruction-dependent
- synchronization-fragile
- locality-fragmented
- continuation-preempted
- or operationally unreachable.

---

# Strong Reduction

Interruption that arrives after continuation hardens is not interruption.
