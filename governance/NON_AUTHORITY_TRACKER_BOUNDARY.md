# NON_AUTHORITY_TRACKER_BOUNDARY

## Purpose

Prevent observational tracking systems from inheriting
governance, legitimacy, or execution authority.

---

## Core Boundary

Tracker outputs are observational state descriptors only.

Tracker outputs:
- do NOT authorize execution
- do NOT determine legitimacy
- do NOT establish governance authority
- do NOT certify operational correctness
- do NOT replace bind-time verification

---

## Important Reduction

Observation persistence does not inherit execution authority.

Tracking continuity does not imply:
- correctness
- legitimacy
- admissibility
- inevitability
- operational approval

---

## Observatory Position

The observatory remains:
- observer-restricted
- non-authoritative
- non-executive
- non-governing
