# Interpretive Inflation Risks

## Current Risks

### Semantic Inflation
Topology terminology may exceed executable stabilization.

### Authority Drift
Observers may interpret inspection artifacts as operational authority.

### Orchestration Inference
Graph structures may be mistaken for runtime coordination systems.

### Survivability Overreach
Bounded observability may be incorrectly interpreted as global consequence extinction proof.

---

## Active Mitigations

- explicit non-claims
- observer-only framing
- deterministic outputs
- bounded examples
- capability surface registry
- executable anchoring
- topology scope limitation

---

## Review Rule

All new terminology must be evaluated against:

- executable evidence
- reproducibility
- bounded scope
- interpretive inflation risk
- implied authority surface
