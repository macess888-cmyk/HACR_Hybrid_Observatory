# CYCLE 001 — INITIAL REDUCTION ATTEMPTS

STATUS: ACTIVE

Objective:

Determine whether Burden Localization explains anything beyond ordinary burden discharge.

---

## Candidate Observation

Two situations may appear identical:

Case A

Burden exists.

Responsible authority exists.

Authority refuses or fails to discharge burden.

Case B

Burden exists.

No responsible authority is localized.

Burden cannot be discharged because no entity is recognized as responsible.

Potential Question:

Are these the same failure?

Or different failures?
