# CVH DISCOVERY BOUNDARY

## Purpose

Clarify the role of CVH (Continuous Validation Harness).

CVH is:
- observer-only
- proposal-generating
- non-authoritative
- controlled-degradation-based

CVH is NOT:
- enforcement authority
- autonomous validator promotion
- governance machinery
- self-authorizing runtime control

---

## Core Function

CVH may:
- induce controlled degradation
- surface unexpected allow paths
- localize bind gaps
- classify candidate failure contours
- propose candidate conditions

CVH may NOT:
- automatically promote candidate conditions
- redefine admissibility
- alter enforcement surfaces autonomously

---

## Promotion Rule

Candidate conditions require:
- reproducibility
- repeated manifestation
- independent verification
- manual review

before promotion into:
- EVH
- IBVH

---

## Bind Trace Scope

CVH bind trace remains proposal-level only.

Candidate classes:
- DISCOVERED_UNPROVEN_BIND
- DISCOVERED_STALE_BIND
- DISCOVERED_REFUSAL_GAP
- DISCOVERED_CARRIED_STATE_PATH
- DISCOVERED_UNKNOWN_SURFACE

These are:
- observational
- provisional
- non-authoritative

until formally promoted.
