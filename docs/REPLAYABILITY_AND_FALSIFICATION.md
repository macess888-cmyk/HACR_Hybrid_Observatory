# HACR Hybrid Observatory
# Replayability and Falsification

Reproducibility / Challengeability / Runtime Inspection

---

## Purpose

This document defines the repository's replayability and falsification posture.

Replayability exists to support inspection and challengeability.

Replayability does not create authority.

---

## Core Principle

Replayability proves inspectability, not admissibility.

A replayable result is still subject to:

- topology incompleteness
- hidden carriers
- replay limitations
- unresolved continuation
- interruption realism constraints
- HOLD-sensitive uncertainty

---

## Replayability Goals

Replayability supports:

- reviewer reproduction
- deterministic inspection
- falsification attempts
- runtime comparison
- continuity visibility
- limitation exposure
- challengeability

Replayability is a diagnostic capability only.

---

## Falsification Principle

The observatory becomes stronger when reviewers can:

- rerun outputs
- challenge assumptions
- expose hidden topology
- test replay inheritance
- test stale-state continuation
- test aggregate containment
- create false PASS conditions
- pressure-test interruption realism

---

## Replay Failure Modes

Replay may fail safely if:

- topology changed
- replay surfaces are incomplete
- hidden carriers exist
- synchronization differs
- stale state persists
- continuation redistributed
- residue remains unresolved

Replay incompleteness preserves HOLD.

---

## Replay != Closure

Successful replay does not prove:

- operational containment
- topology completeness
- admissibility
- legitimacy
- aggregate interruption
- consequence non-survivability

Replay demonstrates reproducibility only.

---

## Unsafe Replay Drift

Replay becomes unsafe if treated as:

- certification
- operational clearance
- admissibility proof
- governance proof
- permission signal
- execution authorization

Replay artifacts are observer-side only.

---

## Reviewer Challenge Path

Reviewers are encouraged to attempt:

- hidden carrier injection
- stale-state restoration
- replay re-entry
- retry survivability
- residue reconstruction
- downstream continuation
- fragmentation persistence
- topology incompleteness exposure

If effect-capable continuation survives:

-> FAIL

If uncertainty remains:

-> HOLD

---

## Diagnostic Boundary

The observatory may support replayable falsification.

It may not replace bind-local proof.

---

## Final Constraint

Replayability strengthens challengeability.

Challengeability strengthens falsifiability.

Falsifiability is stronger than confidence.
