## Interpretation

The progression represents increasing observer-visible environmental visibility.

The progression does not represent:

- prediction
- authority
- governance control
- operational execution

---

## Architectural Trend

The observatory evolved from:

"What exists?"

toward:

"What conditions emerge?"

and then toward:

"How do those conditions change across reviewable observation windows?"
