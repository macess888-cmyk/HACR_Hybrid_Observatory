# Purpose

Previous experiments evaluated explanatory utility.

This experiment evaluates predictive utility.

The objective is not to predict future events.

The objective is to determine whether the continuity ambiguity model can localize likely decision pressure before visible consequences emerge.

↓

# Evaluation Question

Can the model identify locations where future decision pressure is likely to emerge?

---

# Prediction Criteria

A useful prediction should identify:

* competing continuity claims
* evidence conflict
* likely claim selection pressure
* likely decision pressure

before visible consequence occurs.
