# Observations

The compressed candidate appears strongest when:

* continuity ambiguity exists
* multiple continuity claims exist
* claim selection affects decisions
* consequences depend upon decision pathways

The compressed candidate appears weakest when:

* continuity ambiguity is absent
* failure is immediate
* detection is immediate
* correction is immediate
* decision pathways are unaffected

Examples:

* routine maintenance
* simple accounting errors
* obvious classification errors
* simple traffic control failures

---

# Primary Open Question

Can continuity ambiguity exist while claim selection remains irrelevant?

If yes:
