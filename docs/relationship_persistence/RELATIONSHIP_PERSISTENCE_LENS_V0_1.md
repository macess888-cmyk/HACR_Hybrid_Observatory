## Motivation

Recent research cycles repeatedly converged on the possibility that
object-level failure may not be the earliest observable failure surface.

Across governance, institutions, memory systems, authority structures,
continuity systems, and organizational structures, degradation may begin
earlier within relationships and dependencies.

The objective of this lens is not to determine correctness,
admissibility, legitimacy, authority, or consequence ownership.

The objective is to inspect whether constitutive relationships appear
to persist, weaken, fragment, substitute, drift, or disappear over time.

## Core Observation

A description may remain stable while the relationship that originally
made the description meaningful weakens or disappears.

Examples:

Safe
Governed
Trusted
Authorized
Certified
Approved
Operational
Compliant

The description may persist.

The constitutive relationship may not.

---

## Inspection Questions

Are the relationships that support the object still present?

Have critical dependencies changed?

Has substitution occurred without identity review?

Has relationship continuity weakened?

Has relationship continuity strengthened?

Have relationship boundaries shifted?

Can the relationship still be reconstructed?

---

## Relationship Persistence

Candidate Definition:

The degree to which a relationship, dependency, authority linkage,
responsibility linkage, continuity linkage, or other constitutive
connection appears to remain observable, active, and intact over time.

## Detection Problem

Many systems inspect:

- object failure
- output failure
- visible failure

Fewer systems inspect:

- relationship weakening
- dependency substitution
- continuity degradation

The possibility exists that relationship degradation becomes visible
before object-level failure.
