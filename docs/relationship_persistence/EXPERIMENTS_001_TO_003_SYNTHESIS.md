# Comparative Findings

The compressed candidate appeared strongest when:

* multiple continuity claims existed
* evidence conflicted
* decisions differed
* consequences depended on selected interpretations

The compressed candidate appeared weakest when:

* continuity ambiguity was absent
* failures were obvious
* corrections were immediate
* decisions were unaffected

Examples:

* routine maintenance
* simple accounting errors
* traffic control failures
* obvious classification errors
