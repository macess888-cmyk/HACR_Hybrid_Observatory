## Purpose

This template provides an exploratory inspection structure for examining whether a claimed identity remains coupled to the constitutive relationships that make that identity meaningful.

This template does not determine:

- truth
- fault
- liability
- authority
- admissibility
- continuation rights
- governance outcomes

The template only surfaces candidate conditions that may warrant further review.

## Mischaracterization

## Environmental Reorganization

# Observatory Interpretation

A description may persist after the constitutive relationships that originally made that description true have:

- drifted
- been substituted
- been mischaracterized
- been environmentally reorganized
- never fully formed

The Observatory does not determine that condition.

The Observatory may only surface it.
