The compressed candidate weakens if:

* ordinary analysis performs equally well
* added complexity produces no additional insight
* claim selection adds no explanatory value
* predictive utility does not improve

---

# Strengthening Criteria

The compressed candidate strengthens if:

* claim selection localizes decision pressure
* continuity ambiguity predicts disagreement
* explanatory power improves
* predictive utility improves
* decision relevance improves
