# How To Read Continuity Topology Maps

Continuity topology maps are observer-only diagnostic visualizations.

They do not predict failure.
They do not authorize action.
They do not certify operational correctness.

They help make recoverability pressure more inspectable underneath visible continuity.

---

# Center

The center represents visible operational continuity.

A system may appear:

- stable
- measurable
- operational
- governable-looking

while interruption viability and recoverability progressively narrow underneath continuation itself.

Core reduction:

visible continuity != preserved recoverability

---

# Nodes

Nodes represent operational systems, dependencies, workflows, infrastructure surfaces, human coordination layers, or recoverability corridors.

Color indicates recoverability pressure:

- green = more stable recoverability
- orange = narrowing recoverability
- red = recoverability critical

---

# Edges

Solid edges represent visible operational dependencies.

These may include:

- logistics dependencies
- workflow dependencies
- infrastructure pathways
- interruption pathways
- operational coupling

---

# Shadow Dependencies

Purple dashed edges represent lower-visibility hidden coupling.

Examples may include:

- undocumented workarounds
- manual compensation
- invisible recovery assumptions
- hidden survivability concentration
- latent operational dependencies

Core reduction:

distributed appearance != distributed survivability

---

# Drift Progression

Drift progression visualizes how recoverability may narrow over time while visible continuity remains stable.

Core reduction:

stable operation != stable recovery capacity

---

# Human Oversight Boundary

The oversight layer visualizes whether:

- interruption authority
- decision visibility
- manual recovery capability

remain operationally reachable.

---

# UNKNOWN -> HOLD

When interruption viability, recoverability integrity, or localization capability cannot be sufficiently bounded, the observatory resolves toward HOLD rather than certainty.
