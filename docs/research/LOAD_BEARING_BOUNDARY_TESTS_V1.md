Provide a framework for evaluating whether a boundary merely exists or continues to constrain transition under pressure.

# Research Question

What makes a boundary load-bearing?

A boundary may:

Exist

Persist

Remain visible

Remain documented

Yet still fail to constrain transition.

The purpose of this review is to separate:

Boundary Presence

from

Boundary Effectiveness

and

Boundary Load-Bearing Capacity.
