Status: RESEARCH

Promotion Status: NONE

Purpose:

Investigate what changes when a boundary gains, loses, strengthens, weakens, or preserves constraining power.

This document does not assume that a single state-change mechanism exists.

The objective is to determine whether recurring state-change patterns can be identified across boundary failure and persistence trajectories.
