Purpose:

Compare boundary failure trajectories against boundary persistence trajectories.

The objective is not to establish a survivability theory.

# Research Question

Why do some boundaries fail while others continue constraining transition?

A boundary may experience:

Pressure

Replay

Repeated evaluation

Environmental change

Authority ambiguity

Institutional pressure

Yet outcomes differ.

Some boundaries weaken.

Some boundaries persist.

This review examines possible differentiating conditions.
