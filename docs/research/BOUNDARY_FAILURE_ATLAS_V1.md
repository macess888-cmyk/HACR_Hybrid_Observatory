Collect and organize observed boundary failure modes discovered across the Protected Investigability, Load-Bearing Boundary, and CARE-related research programs.

# Research Question

How do boundaries lose their ability to constrain transition?

A boundary may:

Exist

Persist

Remain visible

Remain documented

Yet still fail.

The purpose of this atlas is to localize recurring failure patterns.
