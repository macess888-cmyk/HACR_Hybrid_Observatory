# Observed Failure Patterns

The observatory repeatedly localizes several recurring operational asymmetries across infrastructure, runtime systems, organizations, ecology, and continuity environments.

---

## Core Reductions

### Visible continuity != preserved recoverability

Systems may remain operational while interruption viability degrades underneath continuation.

---

### Coherence != grounding

A system may remain internally coherent while drifting from its original grounding conditions.

---

### Observability != authority

Inspection does not grant governance legitimacy or execution authority.

---

### Manual compensation != resilience

Humans often stabilize fragmented systems manually while hidden recoverability pressure accumulates.

---

### Continuation != legitimacy

Persistence alone does not prove correctness, validity, or ethical grounding.

---

### Replayability != correctness

A reproducible process may still inherit flawed assumptions or degraded locality.

---

### Visibility != governance

Dashboards, logs, metrics, and audit surfaces do not guarantee executable control or meaningful interruption capability.

---

## Purpose

The observatory does not eliminate these pressures.

It localizes them under bounded and reproducible inspection conditions.
