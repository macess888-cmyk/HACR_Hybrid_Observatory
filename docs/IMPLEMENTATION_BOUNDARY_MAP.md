# HACR Hybrid Observatory
# Implementation Boundary Map

Observer-Only / Runtime-Local / Non-Authoritative

---

## Purpose

This document defines where the observatory may support bounded inspection and where it must not become authority infrastructure.

---

# Safe Diagnostic Support Areas

The observatory may support visibility into:

- retry survivability
- replay paths
- stale-state continuation
- interruption timing
- escalation continuity
- dependency survivability
- operational residue
- distributed continuation
- workflow interruption visibility
- human traversability conditions
- support reachability
- topology-aware continuation visibility
- hidden dependency exposure
- replayable falsification

These remain diagnostic-only surfaces.

---

# Unsafe Usage Areas

The observatory must not become:

- execution authority
- governance authority
- certification infrastructure
- automated approval system
- legal decision system
- medical authority
- psychological authority
- custody determination infrastructure
- policy enforcement system
- automated denial infrastructure
- bind substitute
- admissibility proxy

---

# Engineering Boundary

The observatory may:

- expose runtime pressure
- support reviewer inspection
- assist reproducibility
- expose survivability conditions
- map continuation visibility

The observatory may not:

- authorize continuation
- validate legitimacy
- determine correctness
- replace operational bind requirements
- create execution permission

---

# Human Runtime Boundary

The observatory may inspect:

- traversability
- interruption realism
- support reachability
- burden topology
- continuity fragmentation
- exhaustion pressure
- dependency visibility

The observatory may not determine:

- moral worth
- legal merit
- psychological truth
- institutional correctness
- human legitimacy

---

# Observer Dependency Boundary

The observatory must remain:

- removable
- bounded
- challengeable
- reproducible
- non-central
- non-consumable by execution

If operational continuity reorganizes around observer persistence:

-> boundary failure

---

# Translation Boundary

Translation may preserve:

- operational visibility
- replayability
- topology exposure
- interruption semantics

Translation may not assume:

- semantic equivalence
- authority equivalence
- governance equivalence
- legitimacy equivalence

translation != semantic equivalence

---

# Final Constraint

Portable pressure surface, not portable authority.

The observatory exists to support bounded runtime inspection under controlled conditions.

It does not exist to become execution infrastructure.
