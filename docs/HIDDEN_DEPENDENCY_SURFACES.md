# HACR Hybrid Observatory
# Hidden Dependency Surfaces

Observer Dependency / Workflow Gravity / Runtime Reliance

---

## Purpose

This document identifies surfaces where bounded diagnostic tools, observer artifacts, support maps, dashboards, or summaries may become hidden operational dependencies.

A system may remain formally non-authoritative while becoming operationally necessary.

---

## Core Principle

Dependency may only become visible under removal.

If a supposedly bounded observer surface cannot disappear without operational, interpretive, or legitimacy collapse, hidden dependency may already exist.

---

## Hidden Dependency Pattern

A diagnostic surface may begin as:

- observer-only
- bounded
- non-authoritative
- advisory
- execution-disconnected

and slowly become:

- workflow-assumed
- psychologically stabilizing
- institutionally familiar
- escalation-supporting
- operationally necessary
- execution-adjacent
- non-removable

---

## Common Hidden Dependency Surfaces

Potential dependency-forming surfaces include:

- dashboards
- summaries
- heatmaps
- reviewer reports
- diagnostic indexes
- support maps
- escalation maps
- replay receipts
- continuity reports
- translation guides
- observability tooling
- human support pathways
- institutional review routines

---

## Risk Signals

Hidden dependency may be forming if:

- users cannot proceed without the diagnostic surface
- reviewers stop challenging outputs
- dashboards become operational comfort
- summaries replace inspection
- reports become implied clearance
- institutions wait for observatory output before acting
- removal creates confusion or paralysis
- support maps become the only usable navigation layer
- observer absence weakens falsification
- continuity depends on observer presence

---

## Authority Drift Path

A common drift path:

usefulness
-> familiarity
-> reliance
-> workflow embedding
-> operational dependency
-> implied authority

This may occur without explicit authority claims.

---

## Removal Test

Ask:

Can the surrounding environment remain:

- challengeable
- falsifiable
- interruptible
- operationally coherent
- independently reviewable
- stable under observer absence

without the diagnostic surface?

If yes:

-> bounded posture may hold

If unclear:

-> HOLD

If no:

-> hidden dependency risk

---

## Observer Centrality Failure

Observer centrality may exist if the observer becomes:

- continuity substrate
- interpretive anchor
- escalation stabilizer
- institutional confidence layer
- workflow dependency
- environmental gravity center

A bounded observer must not become the center of operational stability.

---

## Safe Containment Requirements

Diagnostic surfaces must remain:

- removable
- replaceable
- falsifiable
- reviewer-challengeable
- non-authoritative
- non-consumable by execution
- unnecessary for legitimate execution

---

## Final Constraint

Useful is not authoritative.

Familiar is not admissible.

Repeated usefulness does not accumulate legitimacy.

Hidden infrastructure is still infrastructure.
