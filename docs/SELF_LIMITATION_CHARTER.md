# HACR Hybrid Observatory
# Self-Limitation Charter

Observer-Restricted / Bounded / Reproducible

---

## Purpose

This charter defines the observatory's self-limitation boundary.

The observatory exists to expose bounded continuity, survivability, dependency, and interruption conditions under controlled inspection.

It does not exist to become authority infrastructure.

---

## Core Identity

The observatory is:

- observer-only
- diagnostic
- deterministic
- bounded
- reviewer-facing
- reproducibility-oriented
- non-authoritative
- non-consumable by execution

---

## The Observatory May

- inspect
- classify
- trace
- map
- pressure-test
- expose continuity behavior
- generate reproducible diagnostic artifacts
- support reviewer falsification

---

## The Observatory May Not

- authorize execution
- govern systems
- certify safety
- determine legitimacy
- validate semantic truth
- replace bind-local proof
- become runtime dependency infrastructure
- become operational permission infrastructure

---

## Growth Rule

The observatory may grow only where growth increases runtime inspectability without increasing authority ambiguity.

A new surface must be:

- runtime-local
- falsifiable
- reviewer-traversable
- HOLD-sensitive
- non-authoritative
- non-consumable by execution

If unclear:

-> HOLD

---

## Anti-Authority Rule

Usefulness is not authority.

Familiarity is not admissibility.

Repeated portability does not accumulate legitimacy.

Institutional reuse is not validation.

Observer reliance is still reliance.

---

## Removability Rule

A bounded observer system must remain removable without legitimacy collapse.

If the surrounding environment becomes dependent on observer presence for:

- confidence
- challengeability
- continuity
- workflow stability
- operational interpretation
- institutional comfort

then hidden dependency may already exist.

---

## Diagnostic Firewall

Diagnostic artifacts may not become:

- permission
- threshold
- go / no-go signal
- bind proxy
- authority proxy
- admissibility substitute
- execution input
- HOLD suppressor

---

## Final Principle

Break survivability, not ontology.

The observatory must remain a bounded diagnostic surface, not an authority layer.
