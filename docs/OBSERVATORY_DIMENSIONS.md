# HACR Hybrid Observatory

## Observatory Dimensions

Observer-only runtime continuity inspection.

The HACR Hybrid Observatory currently operates across four observer-visible dimensions.

---

## 1. Structure

Question:

What exists?

Examples:

- topology
- regions
- corridors
- dependencies
- architecture

Purpose:

Provide visibility into repository structure.

---

## 2. Dynamics

Question:

How does it interact?

Examples:

- traversal
- pressure
- continuity signals
- reduction paths
- dependency movement

Purpose:

Provide visibility into interaction and continuity formation.

---

## 3. Environment

Question:

What conditions emerge?

Examples:

- climate fields
- ecology fields
- density basins
- continuity terrain
- semantic weather

Purpose:

Provide visibility into environmental conditions emerging from repository structure.

---

## 4. Time

Question:

How does it change?

Examples:

- replay windows
- temporal ecology
- observation epochs
- historical drift
- replay visibility

Purpose:

Provide visibility into change across reviewable observation windows.

---

## Observer Limitation

These dimensions remain:

- observer-only
- non-authoritative
- non-governing
- non-executing

They do not imply:

- prediction
- operational truth
- legitimacy
- authority
- governance validity

UNKNOWN → HOLD.
