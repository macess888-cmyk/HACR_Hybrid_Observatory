# HACR Hybrid Observatory
# Diagnostic-to-Execution Firewall

Observer Output / Non-Consumability / Anti-Authority Boundary

---

## Purpose

This document defines the firewall between observatory diagnostics and execution-capable systems.

The observatory may expose diagnostic visibility.

It may not provide execution authority.

---

## Core Rule

Diagnostic artifacts must remain non-consumable by execution.

No report, receipt, index, heatmap, score, label, or summary may become:

- permission
- authorization
- threshold
- bind proof
- admissibility proof
- governance proof
- operational clearance
- continuation input

---

## Unsafe Drift Path

A common authority leak path is:

visibility
-> confidence
-> permission
-> operational substitution
-> execution dependency

This path must be blocked.

---

## Firewall Requirements

Observatory outputs must remain:

- observer-side
- diagnostic
- bounded
- non-authoritative
- non-executable
- non-permissioning
- non-binding
- removable

---

## Forbidden Consumption Patterns

Execution systems must not consume observatory outputs as:

- go / no-go signals
- routing authority
- deployment approval
- safety certification
- escalation permission
- recovery legitimacy
- admissibility substitute
- refusal override
- HOLD suppression

---

## Safe Use Patterns

Safe uses include:

- reviewer inspection
- reproducibility checking
- falsification attempts
- topology visibility
- diagnostic comparison
- limitation documentation
- non-authoritative engineering discussion

---

## Heatmaps, Indexes, and Summaries

Heatmaps, indexes, dashboards, and summaries are especially high-risk.

They may support visibility.

They may not become:

- thresholds
- operational scores
- compliance indicators
- safety indicators
- authorization signals
- confidence substitutes

---

## Recovery Warning

Recovery may restore function.

Recovery does not restore admissibility.

Resumed operation is not fresh bind.

Operational continuity is not legitimacy continuity.

---

## HOLD Protection

If a diagnostic artifact reduces uncertainty visibility or suppresses HOLD:

-> boundary failure

If execution treats diagnostic output as proof:

-> boundary failure

---

## Final Constraint

Diagnostics may witness.

They may not bind.

The observatory must remain outside execution authority.
