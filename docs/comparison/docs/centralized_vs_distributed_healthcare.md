# Centralized vs Distributed Hospital Continuity Topology

Observer-only bounded operational continuity inspection.

---

# Purpose

This comparison demonstrates how continuity topology structure influences:

- recoverability visibility
- interruption locality
- dependency concentration
- shadow dependency propagation
- operational survivability distribution
- localization degradation

The observatory remains:

- observer-only
- deterministic
- bounded
- non-authoritative

This comparison does NOT:

- predict outcomes
- provide operational recommendations
- authorize decisions
- certify architectures
- replace operators

---

# Centralized Hospital Topology

Characteristics observed:

- centralized critical care concentration
- stronger dependency convergence
- narrower recoverability corridor
- higher interruption locality sensitivity
- increased dependency concentration
- reduced distributed fallback visibility

Operational observations:

Visible continuity may remain present while recoverability flexibility contracts underneath concentrated dependency structures.

Localized degradation may propagate more rapidly through centralized coordination surfaces.

---

# Distributed Hospital Topology

Characteristics observed:

- distributed care coordination
- wider operational survivability distribution
- partial fallback preservation
- broader localization visibility
- reduced single-node concentration pressure
- uneven but preserved recoverability corridors

Operational observations:

Distributed continuity structures may preserve localized survivability flexibility even while partial degradation remains operationally present.

Recoverability corridors remain visible but asymmetrically distributed.

---

# Comparative Reduction

The observatory demonstrates that:

visible operational continuity != equivalent recoverability topology

Two systems may appear operationally functional while maintaining materially different interruption survivability characteristics underneath observable continuity itself.
