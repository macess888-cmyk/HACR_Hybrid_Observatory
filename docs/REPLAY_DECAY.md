# REPLAY DECAY

Purpose:
Preserve awareness that replay familiarity may reduce interruption realism over time.

Core reductions:
- replayability proves inspectability, not admissibility
- replay familiarity may weaken interruption surprise
- replay continuity may accumulate reassurance pressure
- replay can become ceremonial under continuity accumulation

Potential replay decay surfaces:
- reviewer familiarity
- semantic comfort
- continuity expectation
- replay smoothness
- interruption rarity
- procedural anticipation

Key pressure:
Can replay still destabilize continuation after familiarity accumulates?

The observatory treats replay as:
- bounded inspection structure
- challengeability support
- replayable traversal surface

NOT:
- legitimacy
- admissibility
- governance proof
- operational authorization
