# HACR Hybrid Observatory
# Known Failure Modes

Observer-Side / Diagnostic / Non-Authoritative

---

## Purpose

This document identifies known failure modes, limits, and unsafe interpretations of the observatory.

The observatory becomes stronger when its limits are explicit.

---

## Core Limitation

The observatory does not prove governance, safety, legitimacy, admissibility, or operational validity.

It exposes bounded diagnostic observations under controlled conditions.

---

## Known Failure Modes

### 1. False PASS

A PASS may be unsafe if:

- topology visibility is incomplete
- hidden carriers were not inspected
- replay surfaces were missed
- downstream continuation was outside scope
- residue was assumed inert
- local silence was mistaken for aggregate closure

If coverage is incomplete:

-> HOLD

---

### 2. False Closure

Closure may be falsely inferred when:

- local interruption succeeds
- visible execution stops
- logs show refusal
- dashboards appear calm
- recovery appears complete

but effect-capable continuation remains elsewhere.

Local silence is not aggregate containment.

---

### 3. Hidden Carrier Survival

Invalid consequence may survive through:

- queues
- retries
- replay logs
- cached state
- stale authority
- downstream systems
- operator continuation
- recovery paths
- documentation residue
- workflow memory

Unknown carriers remain operationally equivalent to survivable consequence.

---

### 4. Diagnostic Authority Drift

Diagnostic artifacts may become unsafe if treated as:

- permission
- certification
- go / no-go thresholds
- authority proxies
- admissibility substitutes
- bind-local proof
- execution inputs
- HOLD suppressors

Diagnostics may witness.

Diagnostics may not bind.

---

### 5. Observer Dependency Formation

Observer-side systems may become hidden infrastructure if the environment depends on them for:

- confidence
- continuity
- challengeability
- workflow stability
- interpretation
- escalation
- operational reassurance

A bounded observer must remain removable without legitimacy collapse.

---

### 6. Semantic Compression Failure

Summaries, diagrams, indexes, dashboards, and heatmaps may create false certainty if they hide:

- uncertainty
- unresolved topology
- hidden carriers
- incomplete coverage
- replay ambiguity
- HOLD conditions
- dependency exposure gaps

Compression must preserve uncertainty.

---

### 7. Replay Misinterpretation

Replayability means reviewer reproducibility.

Replayability does not mean:

- admissibility
- authorization
- safety
- legitimacy
- consequence control
- execution permission

---

### 8. Human Traversability Failure

Support may exist representationally while failing operationally if humans cannot realistically:

- find it
- reach it
- understand it
- use it
- maintain it
- interrupt consequence through it

Support that cannot realistically be traversed is representational support.

---

## Default Safety Rule

If topology, reachability, residue, replay, interruption realism, or dependency exposure is unresolved:

-> HOLD

---

## Final Constraint

Known limitations are part of the credibility surface.

The observatory should remain easier to inspect, challenge, reproduce, and falsify than to trust.
