# LOCALIZED DISAGREEMENT

Purpose:
Preserve bounded disagreement without collapsing replayability or requiring authoritative resolution.

Core reductions:
- disagreement may remain localized
- replay continuity may survive unresolved disagreement
- bounded contestability is healthier than forced convergence
- disagreement does not imply inspection invalidity

Healthy disagreement surfaces may include:
- FIRST-MOVING SURFACE
- MOVEMENT STABILITY
- SEQUENCE OF OBSERVED MOVEMENT
- REPLAY INTERPRETATION

This observatory does not:
- adjudicate correctness
- enforce convergence
- resolve authority
- replace reviewer judgment

Localized disagreement is treated as:
- replay-visible
- topology-local
- bounded
- reviewable
- contestable

NOT:
- governance authority
- hidden-state proof
- operational certification
