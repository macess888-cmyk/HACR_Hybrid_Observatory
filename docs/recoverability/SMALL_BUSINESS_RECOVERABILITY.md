# Small Business Recoverability

The observatory can also be applied to local operational continuity pressure.

Examples include:

- supplier dependency concentration
- delivery-chain fragility
- staffing continuity pressure
- inventory synchronization gaps
- operational reconvergence bottlenecks
- hidden compounding cost pressure
- recovery corridor narrowing

---

## Key Reduction

Continued operation != preserved recoverability

A business may remain operational while:

- recovery capability degrades
- supply flexibility narrows
- staffing resilience weakens
- interruption survivability collapses
- dependency concentration increases

without immediate visible failure.

---

## Purpose

The observatory does NOT manage businesses.

It only localizes:

- continuity pressure
- dependency persistence
- recoverability degradation
- operational bottlenecks
- interruption realism
