## Short Description

The HACR Hybrid Observatory builds bounded observability and recoverability inspection tools for complex operational systems.

It helps visualize where visible continuity may mask hidden recoverability pressure, dependency concentration, interruption fragility, or human oversight narrowing.

## Core Reduction

visible continuity != preserved recoverability

## What The Demos Show

- continuity pressure
- recoverability narrowing
- dependency persistence
- shadow coupling
- interruption fragility
- human oversight pressure

## Boundary

The observatory does not predict, certify, govern, or authorize intervention.

All outputs remain observer-only, diagnostic-only, deterministic, reproducible, and externally reviewable.
