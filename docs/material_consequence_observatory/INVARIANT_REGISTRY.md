# Material Consequence Observatory — Invariant Registry

Status: Experimental v0.3
Mode: Observer-only inspection
Control posture: UNKNOWN → HOLD

---

## Purpose

This registry records candidate invariants that have survived repeated pressure across multiple domains.

An invariant is not treated as truth.

An invariant is treated as a pressure survivor.

Promotion requires repeated survival across:

* material systems
* infrastructure
* organizations
* governance
* forecasting
* AI systems
* accountability systems
* recoverability conditions
* formation-precondition analysis

The registry does not grant authority.

It records what continues to survive inspection.

---

## Operating Constraint

The observatory does not decide.

The observatory does not authorize.

The observatory does not prescribe intervention.

The observatory localizes pressure, divergence, and consequence visibility.

UNKNOWN → HOLD.

# Primary Stabilized Invariants

## Presence ≠ Integrity

A thing may remain present while losing the integrity required for it to continue functioning as assumed.

Examples:

* a bridge remains standing while internal fatigue grows
* a certificate remains present while trust conditions degrade
* an organization remains staffed while coordination fails
* a governance process remains documented while accountability disappears

Status: STABILIZED

---

## Symptom ≠ Condition

A symptom may indicate pressure, but it is not equivalent to the underlying condition producing it.

Examples:

* visible cracking ≠ full structural condition
* public controversy ≠ institutional failure mode
* model output error ≠ system-level cause
* operational delay ≠ root capability loss

Status: STABILIZED

---

## Continuity ≠ Health

A system may continue operating while its health degrades.

Continued operation does not prove resilience, safety, recoverability, or coherence.

Examples:

* infrastructure keeps running while maintenance capacity collapses
* an organization continues activity while strategic coherence disappears
* AI systems continue producing outputs while grounding weakens
* governance processes continue while authority localization degrades

Status: STABILIZED

---

## Persistence ≠ Function

A system, artifact, institution, or representation may persist after its original function has degraded or disappeared.

Examples:

* policy remains but no longer controls behavior
* dashboard remains but no longer reflects operational reality
* certificate remains but no longer preserves trust
* team remains but no longer coordinates effectively

Status: STABILIZED

---

## Representation ≠ Reality Condition

A representation of a condition is not the condition itself.

Examples:

* metric ≠ performance
* certificate ≠ trust
* compliance document ≠ accountability
* explanation ≠ authority
* model confidence ≠ verification

Status: STABILIZED

---

# Formation Preconditions Invariants

## Condition ≠ Formation Preconditions

A condition may remain observable while the prerequisites required to continue producing that condition have already degraded.

Examples:

* trust appears present while inventory and renewal ecology fail
* governance appears present while authority preconditions degrade
* organizational capability appears present while coordination capacity collapses
* confidence appears present while external grounding weakens

Status: UNDER ACTIVE VALIDATION

---

## Observed Condition ≠ Condition-Producing Structure

A visible condition may persist after the structures producing that condition have begun to degrade.

This is a primary cross-domain survivor from the public pressure cycle.

Examples:

* certificate present ≠ trust preserved
* compliance present ≠ accountability preserved
* capability present ≠ coherence preserved
* confidence present ≠ verification preserved
* consensus present ≠ coherence preserved

Status: PROMOTED CANDIDATE INVARIANT

---

## Visible Failure ≠ Earliest Failure

The first observable failure is often not the first failure.

Failure frequently becomes visible only after degradation has already occurred in less-visible supporting layers.

Examples:

* certificate expiration may reveal prior inventory failure
* governance breakdown may reveal prior authority-precondition failure
* execution failure may reveal prior transition failure
* consequence failure may reveal prior formation failure
* recursive confidence failure may reveal prior reality-contact failure

Status: PROMOTED CANDIDATE INVARIANT

# Recursive and Epistemic Invariants

## Confidence ≠ Verification

Confidence may emerge through repetition, recursion, authority, incentives, or reinforcement.

Confidence alone does not verify correspondence with reality.

Status: PROMOTED CANDIDATE INVARIANT

---

## Consensus ≠ Coherence

Consensus may emerge through repeated reinforcement.

Coherence requires continued contact with reality, contradiction, correction, and external grounding.

A system may exhibit high consensus while progressively losing coherence.

Status: PROMOTED CANDIDATE INVARIANT

---

## Alignment ≠ Reality Contact

A system may become increasingly aligned with itself, its incentives, its internal conclusions, or its prior outputs while becoming less aligned with external conditions.

Status: ACTIVE REVIEW SURVIVOR

---

## Consistency ≠ Legitimacy

A system may be internally consistent while lacking authority, admissibility, correctness, or material grounding.

Status: ACTIVE REVIEW SURVIVOR

---

## Recursive Reinforcement ≠ Truth

Repeated exposure to the same interpretation may increase confidence without increasing accuracy.

Status: ACTIVE REVIEW SURVIVOR

What appears present, stable, healthy, compliant, coherent, or operational may differ from the property actually preserving that condition.

Examples:

* apparent trust ≠ maintained trust ecology
* apparent resilience ≠ recoverability
* apparent governance ≠ authority preservation
* apparent alignment ≠ reality contact

Status: ACTIVE REVIEW SURVIVOR

## Representation ≠ Generative Process

A representation may describe, display, or preserve a record of a condition without preserving the process that generated or maintains that condition.

Examples:

* receipt ≠ reconstruction
* explanation ≠ authority
* dashboard ≠ reality condition
* mirror ≠ formation
* audit trail ≠ original trust

Status: ACTIVE REVIEW SURVIVOR

## Receipt ≠ Reconstruction

A receipt may preserve evidence that something occurred without preserving the ability to reconstruct the formation conditions, authority chain, or original trust state that produced it.

Status: ACTIVE REVIEW SURVIVOR

Status: ACTIVE REVIEW SURVOR
