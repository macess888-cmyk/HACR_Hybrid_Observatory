# Purpose

The Geometry Collapse Atlas records recurring cases where one property is mistaken for another.

These collapses are important because many consequence failures begin not with the absence of a property, but with the mistaken assumption that one property automatically implies another.

The atlas does not determine correctness.

The atlas records distinctions that repeatedly survive pressure across domains.

Observed pattern:

Property A
appears to imply
Property B

but does not.

Many consequence pathways begin with this collapse.
