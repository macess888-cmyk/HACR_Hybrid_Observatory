# Material Consequence Observatory Non-Claims

The Material Consequence Observatory does not claim to:

- Certify materials
- Approve materials
- Recommend materials
- Optimize materials
- Design materials
- Manufacture materials
- Predict future discoveries
- Provide engineering instructions
- Provide synthesis instructions
- Provide deployment instructions
- Provide weaponization guidance
- Replace experts
- Replace regulators
- Replace environmental review
- Replace safety review
- Replace laboratory testing

## What It Does

It provides observer-only consequence visibility.

It asks:

- What interacts?
- What degrades?
- What persists?
- What becomes exposed?
- What becomes harder to recover?
- What remains unknown?
- When should inspection enter HOLD?

## Boundary

Receipt generation is not governance.

Receipt generation is not certification.

Receipt generation is not permission.

Receipt generation is visibility only.
