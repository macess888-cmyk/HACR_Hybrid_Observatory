# First Inspection Walkthrough

## Example Material

Iron

## Inspection Scope

Low-risk consequence mapping of common corrosion behavior.

## Why Iron

Iron is a safe first example because corrosion is familiar, visible, non-sensitive, and consequence-rich without requiring hazardous capability generation.

## Inspection Chain

```text
Iron
↓
Oxygen and moisture exposure
↓
Corrosion
↓
Surface degradation
↓
Structural weakening
↓
Maintenance requirement
↓
Recoverability assessment
