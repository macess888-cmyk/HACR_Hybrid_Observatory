This document defines the conditions under which the current Material Consequence Observatory research program may be considered bounded, stabilized, and sufficiently complete for its stated scope.

* final truth
* universal validity
* predictive capability
* authority
* governance permission

Research completion only means:

```text

its limits have been documented,
and further progress likely requires new evidence
rather than additional internal theorization.
```
