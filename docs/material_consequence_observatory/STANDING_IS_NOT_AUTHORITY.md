# Definitions

## Evidence

Observable conditions.

Examples:

```text
Historical audits

Continuity surfaces

Material observations
```

---

## Determination

Interpretation of evidence.

Example:

```text
Continuation admitted

Continuation rejected
```

---

## Standing

Assessment that a determination is supportable, reviewable, or admissible for consideration.

Standing answers:

```text
Can this determination be discussed?
```

Standing does not answer:

```text
Must this determination be obeyed?
```

---

## Authority

Authority answers:

```text
Who can bind action?
```

Authority is outside repository scope.
