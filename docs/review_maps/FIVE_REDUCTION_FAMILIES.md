# Five Runtime Reduction Families

## 1. Representation != Execution

Visibility does not automatically create executable interruption capability.

Examples:

- observability != interruptibility
- replay != recoverability
- auditability != intervention

---

## 2. Continuation Survives Passively / Interruption Reconstructs Actively

Continuation may preserve itself automatically while interruption must be actively rebuilt.

Examples:

- continuation momentum
- synchronization asymmetry
- interruption reconstruction burden

---

## 3. Locality != Visibility

Seeing a system does not guarantee executable interruption locality exists.

Examples:

- distributed continuation
- orchestration locality collapse
- remote override illusion

---

## 4. Survivability != Legitimacy

Operational persistence does not automatically prove legitimacy or governability.

Examples:

- continuity camouflage
- operational coherence
- resilience mythology

---

## 5. Clarity != Smoothness

Visual smoothness does not automatically prove operational clarity.

Examples:

- dashboard coherence
- renderer smoothness
- onboarding smoothness
- replay reassurance

---
