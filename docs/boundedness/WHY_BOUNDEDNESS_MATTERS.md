# Why Boundedness Matters

The HACR Hybrid Observatory intentionally remains bounded.

This is not a limitation accident.

It is a structural safety constraint.

---

## Why

Unbounded observability systems tend to drift toward:

- authority expansion
- semantic inflation
- governance overreach
- behavioral interpretation
- optimization absolutism
- non-falsifiable abstraction

The observatory intentionally avoids these conditions.

---

## Core Principle

Observability != authority

Continuity != legitimacy

Coherence != correctness

Visibility != governance

---

## Why Observer-Locality Matters

Observer-local inspection preserves:

- independent reviewability
- falsifiability
- reproducibility
- bounded interpretation
- external verification

without collapsing into operational authority.

---

## Structural Constraint

Bounded observability may reduce total visibility.

However:

total visibility itself may become unsafe.

The observatory therefore prioritizes:

bounded inspection,
controlled scope,
and recoverable interpretation surfaces.
