# Continuation Infrastructure Lens

## Purpose

The Continuation Infrastructure Lens inspects whether operational continuation
has accumulated survivability mass across infrastructure surfaces such that:

visible interruption
≠
operational interruption.

The lens remains:

- observer-only
- bounded
- replay-oriented
- interruption-local
- non-authoritative
- non-consumable by execution

The lens does not determine legitimacy, governance validity, or authority.

It only inspects whether continuation remains operationally survivable after visible interruption.

---

## Core Pressure

Can interruption still realistically occur
after continuation becomes infrastructure?

---

## Inspection Surfaces

Potential continuation survivability surfaces may include:

- compute infrastructure
- energy dependency
- networking persistence
- coordination continuity
- logistics continuity
- operational memory
- infrastructure distribution
- economic dependence
- institutional embedding
- supply-chain survivability
- replay-capable continuation
- continuation mass accumulation

---

## Key Distinction

Formal interruption may remain visible while:

- continuation survivability persists
- interruption cost increases
- operational inertia accumulates
- dependency mass grows
- continuation remains replay-capable

Visible interruption is not guaranteed operational interruption.

---

## Example Pressure Directions

The lens may inspect whether:

- interruption remains operationally reachable
- continuation persists through distributed infrastructure
- dependency accumulation alters interruption realism
- replayability survives visible refusal
- interruption viability thins under coordination persistence
- continuation remains operationally survivable despite authority preservation

---

## Non-Claims

The lens does not:

- authorize interruption
- evaluate legitimacy
- predict collapse
- govern infrastructure
- determine policy
- define admissibility
- provide operational enforcement

The lens remains:
observer-only,
bounded,
and replay-oriented.

---

## Compression

Replayability proves inspectability,
not operational closure.

Healthy governance exists only while interruption remains realistically reachable.

Break survivability, not ontology.
