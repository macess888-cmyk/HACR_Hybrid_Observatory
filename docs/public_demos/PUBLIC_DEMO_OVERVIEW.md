# Public Demo Overview

The HACR Hybrid Observatory includes bounded operational demonstrators intended to make continuity pressure more inspectable under deterministic conditions.

The demos are observer-only.

They do NOT:

- predict failure
- govern systems
- authorize intervention
- replace operators
- provide operational command

---

# Current Demonstrators

## Recoverability Corridor Demo

Visualizes how visible operational continuity may coexist with narrowing interruption and recovery capability.

Current outputs include:

- recoverability scoring
- continuity pressure
- dependency fragility
- interruption viability overlays

---

## Continuity Drift Visualizer

Visualizes:

- continuity topology
- dependency concentration

- drift progression
- interruption fragility
- recoverability narrowing
- human oversight pressure

Current outputs include:

- deterministic SVG topology maps
- reproducible render artifacts
- replayable JSON scenario inputs

# Purpose

These demos exist to help localize operational asymmetries that may accumulate underneath stable-looking continuity itself.

Core reduction:

visible continuity != preserved recoverability
