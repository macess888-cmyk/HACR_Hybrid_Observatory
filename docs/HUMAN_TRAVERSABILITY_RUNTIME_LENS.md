# HACR Hybrid Observatory
# Human Traversability Runtime Lens

Runtime Reachability / Operational Support Reality / Human Capacity

---

## Purpose

This document defines the observatory's bounded human-runtime inspection lens.

The focus is operational reachability under degraded human capacity.

---

## Core Principle

Support that cannot realistically be:

- found
- understood
- reached
- traversed
- maintained
- or used

may become representational support rather than operational support.

---

## Runtime-Human Conditions

Real-world interruption capability may degrade through:

- exhaustion
- overload
- illness
- aging
- disability
- administrative burden
- fragmented escalation
- digital exclusion
- dependency fragmentation
- interruption delay
- continuity gaps

These are runtime conditions.

---

## Hidden Infrastructure Risk

Systems may silently depend on:

- family support
- caregiver persistence
- institutional literacy
- emotional endurance
- retry capacity
- self-reconstruction of context
- infinite escalation capacity

without modeling those as runtime dependencies.

---

## Traversability Failures

Operational support may fail when:

- the correct path is unclear
- support exists only representationally
- discovery burden exceeds human capacity
- interruption timing collapses
- continuity fragments between systems
- context must be repeatedly rebuilt
- escalation paths become unreachable

---

## Silence Failure

A person stopping escalation does not prove:

- safety
- resolution
- containment
- restored stability
- absence of need

Runtime silence may represent unresolved survivability conditions.

---

## Human HOLD Conditions

If realistic human traversability is unclear:

-> HOLD

Examples:

- unreachable escalation
- inaccessible support path
- degraded interruption capability
- fragmented continuity
- unresolved burden topology
- hidden caregiver dependency

---

## Observer Boundary

The observatory may expose:

- support reachability
- interruption realism
- traversal burden
- continuity fragmentation
- dependency visibility

The observatory may not determine:

- moral judgment
- institutional legitimacy
- legal authority
- psychological truth
- policy correctness

---

## Final Constraint

Human capacity is runtime reality.

Operational continuation may outscale realistic human interruption capability.

Representational support is not guaranteed operational support.
