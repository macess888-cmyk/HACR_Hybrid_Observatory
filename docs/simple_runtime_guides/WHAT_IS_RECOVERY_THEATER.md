# What Is Recovery Theater?

Recovery theater occurs when systems appear recoverable without preserving clear deterministic repair paths.

A system may appear recoverable because:

- logs exist
- dashboards exist
- replay exists
- tooling exists
- explanations exist

while actual repair traversal remains:

- unclear
- fragmented
- ambiguous
- or difficult to reproduce.

Recovery appearance does not automatically prove recovery reachability.

The observatory pressures whether repair clarity remains reproducible under degraded conditions.
