# Hospital Continuity Observability

## Purpose

This demonstrator visualizes operational continuity pressure
inside healthcare environments under bounded deterministic conditions.

The observatory does not:
- provide medical guidance
- perform operational control
- predict failure
- replace hospital operators
- certify recoverability

The demonstrator only visualizes:

- continuity pressure
- dependency concentration
- recoverability narrowing
- interruption realism
- localization degradation
- hidden operational coupling

## Core Reduction

Visible hospital operation does not necessarily imply preserved recoverability capacity.

Systems may remain:
- operational
- measurable
- governable-looking

while interruption viability and recovery flexibility progressively narrow underneath continuation itself.

## Boundary Conditions

Observer-only.

Deterministic.

Reproducible.

Non-authoritative.
