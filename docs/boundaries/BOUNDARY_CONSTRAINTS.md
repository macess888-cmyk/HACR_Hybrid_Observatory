# Boundary Constraints

The HACR Hybrid Observatory intentionally remains bounded.

---

## The Observatory Does NOT

- govern humans
- rank people
- score behavior
- predict futures
- authorize intervention
- enforce legitimacy
- optimize society
- replace institutions
- execute operational command
- provide sovereign authority

---

## Why

Unbounded observability systems risk drifting toward:

- authority expansion
- semantic inflation
- optimization absolutism
- governance overreach
- consequence blindness

The repository intentionally preserves:

- observer locality
- falsifiability
- bounded interpretation
- reproducibility
- external reviewability

---

## Core Principle

Bounded inspection is intentional.

UNKNOWN -> HOLD
