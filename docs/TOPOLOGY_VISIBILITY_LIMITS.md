# HACR Hybrid Observatory
# Topology Visibility Limits

Incomplete Visibility / Unknown Surfaces / HOLD Preservation

---

## Purpose

This document defines limits introduced by incomplete topology visibility.

Operational closure cannot be safely asserted when topology visibility is incomplete.

---

## Core Principle

Unknown topology is structurally incompatible with operational closure claims.

Incomplete visibility preserves HOLD.

---

## Topology Visibility Failure

A system may appear operationally understood while:

- hidden carriers exist
- downstream consumers remain unknown
- replay surfaces remain unresolved
- retries/fallback remain reachable
- stale state persists
- distributed continuation survives
- delayed synchronization exists
- human continuation paths remain active

Incomplete visibility may create false closure.

---

## Visibility != Containment

Observable surfaces may not represent:

- total topology
- aggregate continuation
- distributed persistence
- operational reachability
- replay survivability
- hidden dependency structure

Visible calm is not topology closure.

---

## Hidden Surface Classes

Unknown surfaces may include:

- retries
- queues
- replay systems
- downstream consumers
- cached state
- replicas
- manual workflows
- institutional memory
- escalation paths
- operational residue
- asynchronous synchronization
- undocumented infrastructure

---

## Topology Drift

Topology visibility may degrade through:

- infrastructure evolution
- undocumented workflow changes
- distributed ownership
- synchronization delay
- stale maps
- partial observability
- organizational fragmentation
- human workaround formation

Visibility may decay over time.

---

## Aggregate Closure Risk

Local interruption does not guarantee:

- aggregate interruption
- replay exhaustion
- residue inertness
- distributed collapse
- downstream invalidation

Closure claims require sufficient topology visibility.

---

## HOLD Requirements

Route to HOLD if:

- hidden surfaces remain possible
- topology coverage is incomplete
- distributed persistence is unresolved
- replay eligibility is unclear
- downstream continuity is unknown
- residue inheritance is unresolved

Unknown topology remains operationally equivalent to survivable continuation.

---

## Diagnostic Boundary

The observatory may expose topology visibility limits.

It may not certify topology completeness.

---

## Final Constraint

Operational closure depends on topology visibility.

Unknown topology preserves uncertainty.

Uncertainty preserves HOLD.
