Observatory Position
The HACR Hybrid Observatory is intentionally observer-local and non-authoritative.

The observatory does not claim:
  • execution sovereignty
  • autonomous governance authority
  • universal runtime legitimacy
  • consequence-boundary ownership
  • or enforcement supremacy
The purpose of the observatory is bounded runtime continuity inspection under reproducible conditions.
