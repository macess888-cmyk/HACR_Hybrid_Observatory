# ANTI-DRIFT CONTINUITY

## PURPOSE

This document defines observer-side anti-drift continuity concepts
used by the HACR Hybrid Observatory.

The focus is not permanence.

The focus is whether continuity remains:
- presently valid
- topology-visible
- recoverable
- interruption-compatible
- transition-legible

under changing conditions.

This document is observational only.

---

# CORE REDUCTION

Systems become more dangerous when continuity persists
after validity silently degrades.

---

# PRESENT-STATE VALIDITY

Observer-side continuity inspection requires:
- present-state visibility
- current relation validity
- topology-local interpretation
- replay-compatible inspection

Assumed continuity is insufficient.

Historical validity does not guarantee current validity.

---

# INVALID FORMATION VS COLLAPSE

## Collapse

A previously stable continuity condition:
- degraded
- destabilized
- fragmented
- or lost recoverability

under stress or topology change.

---

## Invalid Formation

A continuity condition that:
- appeared stable
- propagated operationally
- or persisted institutionally

without ever establishing sufficiently valid foundations.

Some systems fail because they collapse.

Some systems fail because they were never validly stabilized.

This distinction is observational only.

---

# RELATION VALIDITY

Continuity depends not only on entities,
but on whether relations remain:
- observable
- bounded
- interpretable
- presently valid

Loss of relation validity may degrade continuity
before visible collapse appears.

---

# CONTINUITY DRIFT

Continuity drift may include:
- stale-state persistence
- inherited assumptions
- hidden dependency propagation
- replay degradation
- interruption viability loss
- compressed transition visibility
- topology interpretation instability

Drift may remain operationally survivable
before becoming observably visible.

---

# ROLLBACK AND RECOVERABILITY

Observer-side preference:
- bounded recoverability
- rollback visibility
- graceful degradation
- interruption preservation
- safe-state return potential

Rollback does not imply:
- restoration certainty
- operational legitimacy
- guaranteed interruption success

Rollback remains observational only.

---

# OBSERVER BOUNDARY

The Observatory:
- observes
- classifies
- maps
- visualizes
- pressure-tests continuity behavior

The Observatory does not:
- authorize continuity
- validate legitimacy
- operationalize rollback
- govern execution
- replace human responsibility

---

# NON-CLAIM

Anti-drift continuity observations:
- do not guarantee correctness
- do not guarantee recoverability
- do not certify safety
- do not imply execution validity

All observations remain bounded to:
- available evidence
- visible topology
- supplied inputs
- local inspection conditions

Unknown downstream conditions may remain possible.
