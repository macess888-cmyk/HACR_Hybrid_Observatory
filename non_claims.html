Non-Claims Boundary

The HACR Hybrid Observatory is an observer-local runtime continuity inspection surface. It does not claim execution authority, certification, governance control, or consequence-boundary prevention.

The Observatory Does Not Claim

  • sovereign governance authority
  • execution control
  • deployment certification
  • legal or regulatory determination
  • institutional enforcement ownership
  • consequence-boundary prevention
  • runtime validity guarantees
  • AGI containment authority

Observer Scope

HACR focuses on bounded inspection, deterministic replay visibility, continuity localization, interruption realism pressure, inheritance drift, and uncertainty preservation.

Core Distinctions

  • Visibility != enforcement
  • Claims != proof
  • Continuity != coupling
  • Coherence != grounding
  • Replayability != admissibility
  • Artifact density != runtime validity
