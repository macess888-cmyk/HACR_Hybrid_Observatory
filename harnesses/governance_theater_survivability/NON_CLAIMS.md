# Governance Theater Survivability Non-Claims

This harness is observer-restricted.

It may inspect:

- governance visibility
- interruption reachability
- review/action connectivity
- escalation viability
- consequence hardening pressure
- runtime interruption collapse

It does NOT:

- govern
- authorize
- adjudicate
- certify
- judge institutions
- rank governance quality
- determine legitimacy
- infer political truth
- infer intent
- infer hidden coordination
- infer inevitability
- operationalize consequence
- replace institutional authority
- replace bind proof

Governance theater survivability is an observability classification only.

It means:

visible governance remains present
while
material interruption capability may be absent, delayed, disconnected, or practically unreachable.

It does not mean:

- illegitimacy
- corruption
- failure by intent
- political conclusion
- moral authority
- institutional finality

Core locks:

- Visibility is not reachability.
- Governance presence is not executable interruption.
- Measurement is not admissibility.
- Evidence does not create bind.
- UNKNOWN -> HOLD.

Break survivability, not ontology.
