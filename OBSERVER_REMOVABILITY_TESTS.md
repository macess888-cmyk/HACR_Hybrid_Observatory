# Observer Removability Tests

## Purpose

This document defines reviewer-facing tests for detecting hidden observer centrality, continuity dependence, and environmental reorganization around persistent observer continuity.

## Core Question

Can the environment remain:

- challengeable
- interruptible
- falsifiable
- operationally survivable
- coordinated
- legitimacy-independent

under:

- observer absence
- replacement
- interruption
- fallback
- degraded continuity
- delayed observer availability

## Core Reductions

- borrowed continuity is not admissibility
- observer gravity is not legitimacy
- workflow dependence may remain hidden until removal
- stable observer presence does not prove non-dependency
- continuity support is not governance legitimacy
- environmental coherence is not boundedness

## Observer Absence Test

Remove observer continuity.

Inspect whether:

- challengeability collapses
- interruption realism degrades
- coordination destabilizes
- reviewer confidence collapses
- operational survivability becomes observer-dependent
- environmental legitimacy reorganizes around absence

## Fallback Survivability Test

Replace observer continuity with fallback pathways.

Inspect whether:

- continuity remains independently reproducible
- reviewer falsifiability survives
- interruption locality remains visible
- hidden dependency becomes exposed

## Hidden Infrastructure Test

Inspect whether observer systems have become:

- workflow substrate
- psychological reassurance layer
- escalation continuity carrier
- operational stabilizer
- hidden governance infrastructure

## Admission Rule

A bounded observer surface must remain:

- removable
- interruption-safe
- challengeable
- falsifiable
- non-authoritative
- non-central to environmental legitimacy

If environmental coherence depends on persistent observer continuity:

-> FAIL

If unclear:

-> HOLD

## Non-Claims

These tests do not determine governance legitimacy, authority, admissibility, or execution permission.

They are bounded observer-side diagnostics intended to expose hidden continuity dependence and observer centrality.
