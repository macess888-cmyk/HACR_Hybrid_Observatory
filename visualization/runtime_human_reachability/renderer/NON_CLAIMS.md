# Renderer Non-Claims

The Runtime Human Reachability Renderer is an observer-only visualization tool.

It does not:

- govern
- authorize
- enforce
- adjudicate
- certify
- determine legitimacy
- infer hidden authority
- infer inevitability
- determine institutional truth
- replace bind proof
- operationalize consequence

Rendered maps are inspection artifacts only.

They may support:

- review
- replay
- localization
- bounded comparison
- deterministic inspection

They do not create:

- authority
- admissibility
- legitimacy
- obligation
- permission
- consequence

Core boundary:

If rendered conditions cannot be materially measured, reconstructed, or falsified:

UNKNOWN -> HOLD

A rendered map is not authority.

Break survivability, not ontology.
