# Synchronization Pressure Map Specification

The Synchronization Pressure Map renders runtime-human coordination burden under interruption pressure.

The map does not inspect:

- sociology
- political organization
- institutional blame
- collective ideology

Primary visualization object:

Runtime synchronization survivability.

Mapped surfaces may include:

- coordination burden
- synchronization delay
- operator exhaustion pressure
- interruption sustainment burden
- recovery coordination load
- fragmentation across roles or systems

Visual semantics:

PASS:
synchronization remains materially executable.

HOLD:
synchronization conditions cannot be reconstructed or measured.

FAIL:
synchronization burden exceeds practical interruption capability.

Core locks:

- Human fatigue is not consent.
- Coordination burden is not legitimacy.
- Synchronization visibility is not interruption capability.
- UNKNOWN -> HOLD.
