The Core Question

Your AI components may all work individually while the system still fails at the joins.

AI Boundary Review focuses on the relations, transitions, authority, evidence, composition effects, and recovery conditions between otherwise valid-looking parts.

What Is Inspected

Components

What properties of each relied-upon component are actually established?

Relations

Are the joins between components independently supported?

Transitions

What properties are proven eligible to cross each boundary?

Composition

What exists only because components were joined, and was it inspected?

Authority

Does technical capability actually have authority for the consequential action?

Consequence

What happens because the workflow acts, and is that consequence supported?

Recovery

Can consequential state be stopped, reversed, corrected, traced, or reconstructed?

Status Semantics

PASS

The inspected property is sufficiently established within the declared scope and supplied evidence.

HOLD

Available evidence is insufficient to establish the property for the declared use, transition, or consequence.

FAIL

Available evidence positively demonstrates that a declared requirement is not satisfied within the reviewed scope.

HOLD is not accusation, rejection, or a claim that a system is unsafe. It means standing has not yet been sufficiently established.
