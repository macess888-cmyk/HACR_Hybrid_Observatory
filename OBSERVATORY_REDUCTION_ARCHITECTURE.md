# HACR Hybrid Observatory — Reduction Architecture

## Purpose

This document defines the observer-only reduction branch of the HACR Hybrid Observatory.

The reduction branch asks:

What remains after visibility is progressively reduced?

It does not predict.
It does not route.
It does not certify causality.
It does not govern execution.
It does not establish operational truth.

UNKNOWN → HOLD.

---

# Reduction Ladder

Epoch
→ Persistence
→ Skeleton
→ Invariant
→ Fixed Point
→ Anchor
→ Anchor Basin

---

## Persistence

Historical visibility across bounded observation epochs.

Question:

What remained visible?

---

## Skeleton

Structural persistence surviving historical visibility reduction.

Question:

What structure remains after terrain and ridge visibility are reduced?

---

## Invariant

Structure visible across every bounded observation epoch.

Question:

What never disappeared?

---

## Fixed Point

Invariant visibility surviving reduction.

Question:

What remains stable after invariant extraction?

---

## Anchor

Fixed-point stability surviving additional reduction.

Question:

What remains stable after survival has already been established?

---

## Anchor Basin

Concentration of reduction-stable anchors.

Question:

Where does stable historical structure accumulate?

---

# Boundary

Reduction artifacts are not:

- predictions
- routes
- recommendations
- causality proof
- governance authority
- execution instructions

They are bounded historical visibility artifacts only.
