# Differential Collapse Heatmap

## Purpose

This tooling visualizes layered survivability divergence under bounded runtime conditions.

The goal is to demonstrate that:

- representation may survive after interruption collapses
- replay may survive after traversal collapses
- visibility may survive after recoverability collapses
- reassurance may survive after operational control collapses

## Core Reduction

observable
≠ inspectable
≠ traversable
≠ interruptible
≠ destabilizable
≠ recoverable

## Focus

- differential survivability
- layered collapse divergence
- reassurance inheritance pressure
- traversal burden realism
- interruption collapse timing
- recoverability exhaustion
- visual reassurance falsifiability

## Boundary

Observer-only.

Not governance.
Not authority.
Not prediction.
