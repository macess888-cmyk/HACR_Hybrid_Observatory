# Observatory Self-Pressure Check

## Purpose

This adversarial pressure check tests whether the observatory itself begins generating reassurance infrastructure beyond interruption evidence.

## Core Reduction

Observability persistence is not interruption persistence.

## Pressure Questions

Does the observatory:

- appear safer than interruption evidence supports?
- imply recoverability from replay existence?
- imply traversability from visualization clarity?
- imply interruption from inspection continuity?
- imply destabilizability from replay survivability?
- imply recovery from procedural coherence?

## HOLD Condition

HOLD if reassurance exceeds materially reconstructable interruption evidence.

## Boundary

Observer-only.

No operational authority.
