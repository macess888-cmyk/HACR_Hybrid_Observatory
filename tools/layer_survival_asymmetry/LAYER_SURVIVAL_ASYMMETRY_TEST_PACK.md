# Layer Survival Asymmetry Test Pack

## Purpose

This test pack pressures whether survival of earlier continuity layers silently inherits survival assumptions into later operational layers.

## Core Reduction

Layer survival is not layer inheritance.

## Core Layer Chain

observable
↓
inspectable
↓
traversable
↓
interruptible
↓
destabilizable
↓
recoverable

## Primary Pressure Direction

Systems may preserve:

- replay
- dashboards
- procedural coherence
- validator continuity
- synchronization
- audit survivability
- institutional continuity

while materially losing:

- interruption reachability
- destabilizability
- or recovery viability

under bounded runtime conditions.

## Boundary

Observer-only.

No authority.
No governance determination.
No operational execution.
