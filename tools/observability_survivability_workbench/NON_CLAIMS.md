# Non-Claims

The Observability Survivability Workbench:

- does not govern
- does not authorize
- does not certify
- does not adjudicate
- does not determine legitimacy
- does not infer inevitability
- does not operationalize consequence
- does not replace reviewer judgment
- does not replace bind proof

Workbench PASS is not authority.

Workbench FAIL is not legitimacy collapse.

Workbench HOLD preserves bounded uncertainty.

UNKNOWN -> HOLD.
