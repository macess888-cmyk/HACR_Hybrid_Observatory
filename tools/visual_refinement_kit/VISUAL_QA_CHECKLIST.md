# Visual QA Checklist

## Questions

1. Can the reviewer understand the reduction in under five seconds?
2. Is the reduction visible?
3. Is the footer present?
4. Are colors standardized?
5. Is the layout readable?
6. Is the SVG traversal-friendly?
7. Does the visual avoid authority implication?
8. Does the animation remain operationally simple?
9. Is the reduction reproducible from the deterministic case?
10. Does UNKNOWN -> HOLD remain preserved?

## Healthy Compression

Healthy visuals improve:

- traversal
- clarity
- reproducibility
- reviewer ergonomics

without increasing semantic scope.
