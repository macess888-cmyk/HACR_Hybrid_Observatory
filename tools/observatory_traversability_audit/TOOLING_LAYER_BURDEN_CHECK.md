# Tooling Layer Burden Check

## Runtime Concern

Additional tooling layers may increase:

- reviewer fatigue
- traversal complexity
- onboarding overload
- replay fragmentation
- operational ambiguity

## Pressure Direction

more layers
↓
more traversal
↓
more burden
↓
less clarity
↓
more reassurance inheritance risk

## Reduction

More tooling is not more traversability.

## Preserve

Operational clarity under bounded reviewer effort.
