# Machine-Speed Propagation Pressure

## Purpose

This note pressures whether continuation propagation exceeds bounded human interruption and interpretation capacity.

## Core Reduction

Machine-speed continuity can outrun human interruption.

## Pressure Sequence

local instability
↓
propagation acceleration
↓
interpretation compression
↓
reviewer saturation
↓
interruption degradation
↓
consequence hardening

## Strongest Risk

Systems may remain:

- visible
- synchronized
- replayable
- procedurally coherent
- and operationally smooth

while interruption capability becomes non-traversable.

## Boundary

This note remains observer-only and operationally bounded.
