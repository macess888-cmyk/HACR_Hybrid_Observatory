# Interpretation Window Model

## Purpose

This model pressures whether interpretation, escalation, interruption, and recovery windows remain materially reachable before consequence hardens.

## Core Reduction

Interpretation visibility is not interruption capability.

## Runtime Sequence

detect
↓
interpret
↓
localize
↓
escalate
↓
interrupt
↓
recover

## Compression Risk

Machine-speed continuation may compress operational interpretation windows faster than humans can:

- detect
- understand
- escalate
- intervene
- or recover

## Failure Surface

Operational smoothness may survive after human interruption windows collapse.

## Boundary

This model remains observer-only and runtime-local.
