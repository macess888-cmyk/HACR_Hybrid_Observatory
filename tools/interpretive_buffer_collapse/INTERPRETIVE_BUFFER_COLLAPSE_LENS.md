# Interpretive Buffer Collapse Lens

## Purpose

This observer-only lens pressures whether machine-speed continuation begins exceeding bounded human interpretation, interruption, escalation, or recovery capacity before consequence hardens.

## Core Question

Can humans still materially interpret, constrain, interrupt, escalate, or recover before propagation exceeds operational interruption reachability?

## Core Reduction

Human compensation is not system coherence.

## Pressure Surface

Systems may remain:

- operationally smooth
- replayable
- procedurally coherent
- validator-backed
- synchronized
- institutionally continuous
- psychologically governable-looking

while human interruption capacity progressively collapses underneath:

- propagation speed
- synchronization compression
- dependency layering
- reconstruction timing exhaustion
- reviewer traversal overload
- escalation delay
- and continuation pressure

## Boundary

This lens remains observer-only.

It does not determine legitimacy, governance adequacy, operational approval, or execution authority.
