# Glue Hours and Compensation Load

## Purpose

This note pressures hidden human labor maintaining continuity inside fragmented systems.

## Core Reduction

Hidden compensation is not architectural coherence.

## Compensation Behaviors

- manual reconciliation
- runtime translation
- undocumented recovery
- operational improvisation
- dependency bridging
- escalation rerouting
- synchronization repair
- exception localization

## Pressure Surface

Human beings may preserve the appearance of continuity while systems progressively lose interruption compatibility.

## Risk

Organizations may mistake sustained compensation capacity for structural resilience.

## Boundary

This note remains observational and diagnostic only.
