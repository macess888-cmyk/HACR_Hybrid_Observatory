# Public Safe Visual Boundary

## Purpose

This document preserves observer-only interpretation boundaries for visual artifacts.

## Visuals Must Not Imply

- authority
- legitimacy
- inevitability
- governance determination
- operational authorization
- interruption automation
- predictive certainty

## Visuals May Show

- traversal burden
- replay locality fragmentation
- layer degradation
- recoverability exhaustion
- interruption timing pressure
- synchronization degradation
- reviewer traversal realism

## Preserve

Observer-only • Not authority • UNKNOWN -> HOLD
