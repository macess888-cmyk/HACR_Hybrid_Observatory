# Governable-Looking Collapse Gradient

## Purpose

This observer-only lens pressures whether systems continue appearing operationally governable while interruption reachability, destabilizability, and recoverability progressively collapse underneath continuation pressure.

## Core Reduction

Governable-looking is not governable.

## Pressure Direction

Systems may remain:

- replayable
- observable
- synchronized
- procedurally coherent
- validator-backed
- dashboard-stable
- institutionally continuous
- psychologically governable-looking

while materially losing:

- interruption reachability
- destabilizability
- recoverability
- and affected-human restoration viability

under bounded runtime conditions.

## Boundary

Observer-only.

No governance determination.
No legitimacy inference.
No operational authority.
