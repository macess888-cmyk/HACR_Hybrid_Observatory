# Interruption / Destabilization / Recovery Split

## Purpose

This layer pressures whether interruption, destabilization, and recovery survive independently under runtime pressure.

## Core Reduction

Interruption is not destabilization.
Destabilization is not recovery.

## Pressure Sequence

interruptible
↓
destabilizable
↓
recoverable

## Runtime Risk

A system may still permit:

- local interruption
- bounded destabilization
- replay traversal
- procedural response

while affected-human recovery viability progressively collapses.

## Failure Surface

Operational continuity survives after recoverability expires.

## Boundary

Observer-only and runtime-local.
