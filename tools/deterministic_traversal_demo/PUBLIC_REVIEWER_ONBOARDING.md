# Public Reviewer Onboarding

## What This Demo Shows

This demo shows that systems can remain visible, replayable, documented, and procedurally coherent while practical interruption reachability degrades.

## What To Look For

Ask:

1. Can the reviewer traverse the evidence in time?
2. Can the affected person interrupt in time?
3. Does replay remain local enough to reconstruct?
4. Does dashboard stability create false reassurance?
5. Does procedure survive while interruption capability collapses?

## What This Demo Does Not Claim

It does not claim:

- governance authority
- certification
- legal adequacy
- operational approval
- predictive certainty
- system failure in all cases
