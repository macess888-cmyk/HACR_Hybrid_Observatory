# Stabilization Health Score

## Scoring Axes

### Traversal Cost

How difficult is the addition to:

- understand
- reproduce
- review
- traverse
- explain

### Falsifiability Gain

Does the addition:

- improve deterministic evidence?
- improve reproducibility?
- improve failure visibility?
- improve reviewer verification?

### Reassurance Risk

Does the addition increase:

- authority camouflage?
- visual reassurance?
- symbolic coherence?
- onboarding inflation?
- reviewer comfort inheritance?

---

# Healthy Direction

Healthy stabilization:

LOW traversal cost
+
HIGH falsifiability
+
LOW reassurance risk

---

# Runtime Reduction

Healthy maturity reduces operational traversal cost while increasing falsifiability and reducing reassurance inheritance risk.
