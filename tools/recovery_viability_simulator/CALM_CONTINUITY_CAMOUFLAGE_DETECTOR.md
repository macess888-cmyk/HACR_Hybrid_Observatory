# Calm Continuity Camouflage Detector

## Purpose

This tooling pressures whether operational calm survives after recoverability viability collapses.

## Surface Stability Signals

- dashboard stable
- replay available
- validator passing
- synchronization preserved
- procedural coherence preserved
- institutional continuity preserved

## Hidden Collapse Signals

- interruption delay accumulation
- traversal saturation
- escalation fragmentation
- destabilization exhaustion
- recoverability exhaustion
- affected-human pathway collapse

## Core Runtime Risk

Systems may appear operationally stable while recoverability progressively collapses underneath continuation pressure.

## Strongest Reduction

Smooth continuity can outlive recoverable control.

## Boundary

Observer-only and operationally bounded.
