# Observatory Recovery Self-Pressure

## Purpose

This tooling adversarially pressures whether the Observatory itself becomes reassurance infrastructure.

## Runtime Questions

Does:

- replay persistence
- visualization persistence
- dashboard persistence
- validator continuity
- procedural coherence
- recovery acknowledgement
- reviewer visibility
- institutional continuity

silently inherit assumptions of:

- interruption persistence
- destabilization persistence
- or recoverability persistence?

## Strongest Runtime Risk

The observatory itself becomes psychologically reassuring while recovery viability degrades underneath runtime pressure.

## Core Reduction

Recovery visibility is not recovery viability.

## Boundary

Observer-only and anti-authoritative.
