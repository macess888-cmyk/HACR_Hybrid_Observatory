# Layer Survival Heatmap

## Purpose

This heatmap pressures whether earlier continuity layers survive after deeper operational layers collapse.

## Layer Chain

observable
↓
inspectable
↓
traversable
↓
interruptible
↓
destabilizable
↓
recoverable

## Pressure Surfaces

- replay persistence
- synchronization pressure
- reviewer traversal overload
- operational smoothness camouflage
- escalation delay
- replay locality fragmentation
- interruption reconstruction timing exhaustion
- recoverability exhaustion

## Runtime Observation

Earlier layers may survive while later layers collapse.

## Strongest Reduction

Layer survival is not layer inheritance.

## Example Runtime Pattern

observable = survives
inspectable = survives
traversable = partial
interruptible = degraded
destabilizable = degraded
recoverable = collapsed

## Boundary

Observer-only and operationally bounded.
