## Purpose

This tooling pressures whether affected humans retain materially reachable recovery windows before consequence hardens.

- escalation routing burden

## Runtime Failure Pattern

Humans remain visible and acknowledged while recovery pathways become materially unreachable.

## Strongest Reduction
