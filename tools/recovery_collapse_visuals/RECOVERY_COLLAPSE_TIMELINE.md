# Recovery Collapse Timeline

## Purpose

This timeline pressures whether recovery viability collapses while continuity visibility persists.

## Core Reduction

Recovery can collapse before visibility changes.

## Runtime Sequence

t0
- observable
- inspectable
- traversable
- interruptible
- destabilizable
- recoverable

t1
- replay survives
- dashboards remain stable
- synchronization persists

t2
- reviewer traversal burden increases
- replay locality fragments
- escalation routing expands

t3
- interruption windows narrow
- reconstruction timing pressure increases
- affected-human recovery latency accumulates

t4
- interruption remains locally possible
- destabilization becomes partial
- continuation pressure persists

t5
- recovery viability exhausts
- consequence hardens
- restoration pathways fragment

t6
- dashboard remains green
- procedural coherence survives
- institutional continuity survives
- recovery remains materially unreachable

## Strongest Pressure Surface

Systems may appear operationally calm while recoverability has already collapsed.

## Boundary

Observer-only and runtime-local.
