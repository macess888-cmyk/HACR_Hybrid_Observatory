# Interruption / Destabilization / Recovery Diagram

## Purpose

This diagram pressures whether interruption, destabilization, and recovery survive independently under continuation pressure.

## Core Reduction

Interruption is not destabilization.
Destabilization is not recovery.

## Layer Chain

interruptible
↓
destabilizable
↓
recoverable

## Runtime Risk

A system may permit:

- local interruption
- bounded destabilization
- replay traversal
- procedural response

while materially losing:

- recovery viability
- affected-human restoration reachability
- operational restoration timing compatibility

## Failure Pattern

Continuation becomes partially interruptible while recovery becomes operationally unreachable.

## Strongest Reduction

Stopping continuation is not restoring viability.

## Boundary

Observer-only and operationally bounded.
