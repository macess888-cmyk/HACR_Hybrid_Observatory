# Traversability Topology Model

## Purpose

This model visualizes the distinction between:

- observability
- inspectability
- traversability
- interruption reconstructability
- and continuation destabilizability

The model remains observer-only.

It does not govern, authorize, certify, or determine legitimacy.

## Core Reduction

Observable is not traversable.

Traversable is not interruptible.

Interruptible is not destabilizable.

## Traversability Layers

VISIBLE
↓
INSPECTABLE
↓
TRAVERSABLE
↓
INTERRUPTIBLE
↓
DESTABILIZABLE

## Pressure Surface

A system may remain:

- visible
- replayable
- reviewable
- synchronized
- validator-backed
- procedurally coherent

while practical interruption enactability quietly collapses underneath:

- traversal burden
- synchronization degradation
- replay locality fragmentation
- dependency layering
- reviewer overload
- timing exhaustion
- operational smoothness camouflage

## Boundary

The topology only visualizes bounded operational traversal realism.

It does not determine governance adequacy or operational legitimacy.
