# Reviewer Traversal Budget Meter

## Purpose

This instrumentation estimates whether bounded human reviewers can realistically traverse interruption-relevant evidence before consequence hardens.

## Core Reduction

Reviewer visibility is not reviewer traversability.

## Pressure Variables

- replay volume
- dependency count
- synchronization complexity
- semantic density
- timing burden
- reconstruction branching
- operational continuation pressure

## Budget Zones

### PASS

Traversal remains materially achievable.

### HOLD

Traversal realism unresolved.

### SATURATED

Traversal burden exceeds bounded reconstruction realism.

## Risk

Evidence may exist while practical interruption reconstruction becomes non-enactable.

## Boundary

The meter does not determine reviewer correctness or authority.

It only localizes traversal burden realism.
