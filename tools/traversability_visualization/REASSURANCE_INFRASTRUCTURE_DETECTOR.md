# Reassurance Infrastructure Detector

## Purpose

This instrumentation localizes when observability infrastructure begins functioning as symbolic reassurance while executable interruption capability degrades.

## Core Reduction

Reassurance infrastructure is not recoverable control.

## Detection Indicators

- dashboard stability
- replay persistence
- validator continuity
- escalation symbolism
- procedural coherence
- institutional smoothness
- reviewer comfort persistence

## Pressure Surface

Systems may appear increasingly governable while interruption reconstructability progressively collapses underneath continuation pressure.

## Boundary

This detector does not invalidate observability.

It prevents observability from silently inheriting interruption assumptions.
