# Reviewer Ergonomics Check

## Core Question

Can a reviewer reproduce, inspect, and explain the case in under two minutes?

## Traversal Pressure

Measure:

- onboarding clarity
- screenshot readability
- SVG readability
- reduction clarity
- traversal burden
- animation clarity
- replay locality readability
- interruption visibility
- recovery visibility

## Healthy Compression

Healthy visuals reduce:

- semantic overload
- traversal fatigue
- reviewer ambiguity
- authority inheritance risk

without reducing falsifiability.
