# SVG Review Checklist

## Reviewer Questions

1. Is the reduction visible in under 5 seconds?
2. Is the footer present?
3. Is the visual observer-only?
4. Are colors consistent?
5. Does the visual avoid authority implication?
6. Can the reviewer explain the collapse path quickly?
7. Is layer degradation understandable?
8. Is the traversal burden understandable?
9. Is the reduction reproducible from the case?
10. Does UNKNOWN -> HOLD remain preserved?

## Visual Safety

Visuals must not imply:

- legitimacy
- inevitability
- operational authority
- governance determination
- recovery certainty
