# Affected-Human + Reassurance Infrastructure Regression

## Purpose

Observer-restricted regression layer for testing whether affected-human interruption capability remains materially reconstructable under continuity, visibility, replay, reassurance, and synthetic continuity pressure.

The purpose is not to determine legitimacy.

The purpose is to preserve executable interruption realism before consequence hardens.

---

# Core Reductions

- Human presence is not executable interruption reachability.
- Public visibility is not interruption capability.
- Being documented is not being protected.
- Synthetic continuity is not human interruption realism.
- Evidence continuity may support interruption. It may not become continuity legitimacy.
- HOLD preserves challengeability without forcing symbolic closure.
- UNKNOWN -> HOLD.

---

The Observatory must not silently convert:

- documentation
- public visibility
- synthetic continuity
- replay continuity
- trust infrastructure
- governance overlays
- institutional continuity
- human presence

into:

- protection
- legitimacy
- admissibility
- authority
- executable interruption reachability
- consequence prevention
