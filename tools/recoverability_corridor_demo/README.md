# Recoverability Corridor Demo

## Purpose

Demonstrate how operational systems may preserve visible continuity while future interruption and recovery capability progressively narrow.

---

# Example Scenario

A business appears operationally healthy.

However:

- supplier concentration increases
- staffing redundancy decreases
- operational complexity increases
- manual compensation increases
- dependency survivability decreases

while visible continuity remains stable.

---

# Observable Asymmetry

visible continuity != preserved recoverability

---

# Demo Output

The demo visualizes:

- recoverability narrowing
- interruption viability degradation
- hidden dependency accumulation
- continuity pressure concentration
- survivability corridor contraction

under deterministic and replayable conditions.

---

# Why It Matters

Many operational failures emerge gradually underneath stable-looking continuity rather than through immediate visible collapse.
