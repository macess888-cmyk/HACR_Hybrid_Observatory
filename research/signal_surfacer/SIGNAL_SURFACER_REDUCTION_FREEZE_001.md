## First Reduction

Signal Surface is not necessarily detecting drift.

Signal Surface may localize where drift becomes visible.

This distinction matters.

Drift origin and drift visibility may occur at different locations.

---

## Second Reduction

Object Preservation
!=
Relationship Preservation

Objects may remain visible.

Relationships may weaken.

Meaning changes within relationships before objects visibly fail.

---

## Third Reduction

Not all relationships are equal.

Relationships appear separable into:

Descriptive Relationships

Constitutive Relationships

Descriptive relationships describe an object.

Constitutive relationships help define the object.

---

## Fourth Reduction

Identity Persistence

An object may survive loss of descriptive relationships.

An object may not survive loss of constitutive relationships.

Question:

What must survive for an object to remain itself?

---

## Fifth Reduction

Evidence Locality

Evidence may continue accumulating.

Confidence may continue accumulating.

Documentation may continue accumulating.

While constitutive relationships weaken.

Therefore:

Evidence Of Object Persistence

!=

Evidence Of Identity Persistence

---

## Sixth Reduction

Drift Localization

Drift may emerge through:

Dependency weakening

Relationship weakening

Identity weakening

Object weakening

Visible degradation

Most systems inspect the final stage.

Few systems inspect the earlier stages.

## Seventh Reduction

Dependency Structures

Possible deeper primitive:

Dependencies generate relationships.

Relationships generate identity.

Identity generates object continuity.

Potential chain:

Dependency
→ Relationship
→ Identity
→ Object

Open Question:

Are constitutive relationships observable manifestations of deeper dependency structures?
