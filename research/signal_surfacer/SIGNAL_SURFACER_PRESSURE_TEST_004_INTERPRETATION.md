# Test Case D — Interpretation Drift

## Scenario

A public statement is made.

Original statement:

"There may be evidence suggesting a possible relationship between A and B."

Over time the statement becomes:

"There is evidence of a relationship between A and B."

Later:

"A and B are related."

Eventually:

"It is known that A and B are related."
