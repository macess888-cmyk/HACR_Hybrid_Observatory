Purpose

Observer-only signal inspection framework designed to surface:

- hidden signals
- fragmented signals
- suppressed signals
- distorted signals
- delayed signals

The Signal Surfacer does not determine intent.

The Signal Surfacer localizes observable signal conditions and generates intent hypotheses only.
